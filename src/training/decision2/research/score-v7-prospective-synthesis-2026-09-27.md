# Score v7: failure synthesis and prospective evidence-state curriculum

**Status: design draft only.** No v7 corpus, selector, key, model inference,
optimizer update or Hugging Face dataset revision exists under this method.
The Score v6 English direct-LoRA pair is closed as `DO_NOT_ADVANCE`; its r2
selector is consumed. This note uses only the signed aggregate negative
receipt and earlier public-safe audits. It does not reopen r2 item text,
predictions, key, private join, Chinese held material or FINAL.

The last row suggests a **hypothesis**, not a causal conclusion about any
individual case: extra three-level training may correct underuse of level 1
while weakening the evidence needed for level 2. Repeated short templates or
confounding between evidence quantity and sufficiency could contribute.
Only a new design and a fresh independent selector can test that explanation.
Changing the r2 margin from 12 to 11, finding another checkpoint, reusing its
key, or presenting the positive interval as a passed gate is excluded.

## Proposed v7 intervention

Test an **evidence-state transition** curriculum rather than another fixed
count or two-list intersection template. Each case has a rulebook, at least
two independently necessary evidence channels, dated/current-versus-archived
updates, and a scoped disqualifier. The same case supports a complete related
0/1/2 triplet under a frozen explicit rule:

- **0:** a currently applicable disqualifier is proved by the joined sources;
- **1:** no applicable disqualifier is proved, but at least one required
  condition remains genuinely unresolved;
- **2:** no applicable disqualifier is proved and every required condition is
  positively verified by the joined sources.

Crucially, an apparent veto in a single document is insufficient: another
source establishes its scope and current status. The 1-to-2 transition adds
decisive verification, while the 0-to-1 transition removes or supersedes an
operative disqualifier. Keep the rule, case identity, output vocabulary,
number of documents and irrelevant evidence counts fixed within a triplet;
change only the logically operative status relationships. Each source alone
must admit at least two possible answers under an explicit alternative
completion proof. Independent structured and rendered-text oracles must
agree. This design seeks a visible semantic distinction between *unknown*
and *verified*, rather than teaching the middle label as a generic default.

Prospective authoring should use varied realistic procedures and document
forms, not copies or translations of the r1/r2 operation templates. A
feasibility target is four genuinely distinct procedural contexts with about
120 independent groups each: **480 groups / 1,440 rows**, approximately 320
English and 160 Chinese groups, with all three levels per group. This is a
quality ceiling, not a quota: failure of source necessity, shortcut, overlap
or editorial checks blocks the version instead of filling it with weaker
cases. Freeze exact counts, source pool, generator, candidate seed, licenses
and abort thresholds in a separate signed preregistration **before any row is
authored**. No v6 or r2 row may be relabeled or reused.

The first causal training test should keep the direct, byte-pinned original
`BEST368` LoRA-plus-base initialization and fresh optimizer. Arm A receives
the admitted v7 cases plus the identical parent Choice/Noul replay; Arm B
receives token-matched eligible parent Score control plus the same replay.
Both arms must use the same native adapter, seed, optimizer, update count,
maximum length, calibration policy and fixed final checkpoint. Freeze the
admitted **raw and padded** token exposure before GPU use. A separate
ordinal-aware loss or extra endpoint replay could be valuable, but must be a
later independently controlled ablation: adding it only to A would confound
the v7 data hypothesis. The failed v6 A checkpoint is not a new source.

## Data, language and leakage gates before any optimizer

1. Pin all source bytes, parent rights manifest and protected gold-free prompt
   rosters. Screen whole groups for exact and bounded near matches in state,
   instructions and full prompt against parent TRAIN/SELECT/CAL, prior Score
   candidates, r1/r2, typed DEV, CSS, public and authored rosters, and any
   available sealed FINAL prompt inventory. A separate custodian can perform
   this comparison without exposing r2 or protected text to the v7 author.
   Approximate overlap checks cannot certify semantic independence.
2. An independent oracle verifies every 0/1/2 triplet, source necessity,
   temporal applicability, scoped disqualifier and each transition witness.
   Enumerate one-field, one-source, length/count, position, date and lexical
   shortcuts on group-held-out cases. Any feature that perfectly decodes a
   full triplet is a blocker; numeric caps for broader shallow baselines must
   be frozen before construction. Do not repeat v4/v5's failed weighted
   pairing and then relax their cap.
3. Produce a salted, opaque-ID, gold-free review packet only after mechanical
   admission. An independent reviewer solves complete groups and seals
   ambiguity, realism, source necessity and shortcut findings before the
   author key or join is opened. For Chinese, use native-authored cases and a
   documented qualified independent Chinese editor: all admitted Chinese
   TRAIN groups receive a language pass, with a second blind check on a
   prospectively fixed stratified sample and all flagged cases. AI-only
   spot reading cannot certify that gate. If this review is unavailable,
   keep Chinese held and make no bilingual or full-v7 claim.
4. Preserve source-level rights and attribution in the private manifest;
   internally authored additions do not relax the parent corpus terms.
   Keep restricted raw text private. No v7 dataset upload occurs before all
   lineage, rights and language gates pass.

## New selector and stop rule

An independent author, isolated from v7 TRAIN cases and the consumed r2
source/key/predictions, must create **r3** with new procedures and renderings.
One proposed scale is 120 independent triplet groups / 360 rows, stratified
as 72 English and 48 Chinese groups, with complete 0/1/2 balance. Its own
oracle, native Chinese editorial review, source/label separation, opaque
packet, shortcut probes, overlap audit, version hashes and native adapter
must be frozen before any v7 model response. The r3 authors cannot use r2
per-item failures to tune cases. Keep a small separate development smoke
set for implementation; do not spend r3 labels on code debugging.

Before training, sign the exact run and scoring contract, a single planned
final checkpoint, paired group-bootstrap unit, parent Choice/Noul retention
floors, per-language and per-level results, invalid-answer handling, and
the overall gain gate. To avoid easing the failed r2 standard, a possible
new 360-row design would demand **at least 23 more correct** for A than B
(at least the old 6.25% absolute margin), a positive paired group interval,
no material 0/2 endpoint regression in either language, and the prior
parent Choice/Noul retention limits. Exact endpoint floors and the bootstrap
recipe require power/feasibility review and signing **before r3 labels or
model predictions become available to the scorer**. They are not retroactive
gates for v6.

Any mechanical, language, source-parity, optimizer, native-output or r3 gate
failure stops that version. A pass allows one independent DEV/CSS diagnostic
under a separately frozen plan, not a JevArena FINAL or publication claim.
Package parity, full multilingual transfer, release-panel quality and
rights checks remain separate. Preserve the v6 negative result and all old
artifacts unchanged.
