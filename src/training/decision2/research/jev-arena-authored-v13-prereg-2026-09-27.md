# JevArena authored v13: prospective native-valid DEV editorial pilot

**Status: method only.** This protocol is written after the sealed v12 blind
reviews and private aggregate exposed native-answer and source-redundancy
failures, but before any v13 case, target or reviewer packet exists. Versions
v9–v12 stay frozen with their blocked verdicts. This is a small DEV quality
pilot, not a release set, model-selection set or training source. No model
inference, GPU run, protected FINAL access or publication is authorized here.

## Core change

The release benchmark will count **independent originals** as its question
units. Its paired robustness transformations will be scored separately, with
confidence intervals clustered by original case. A perturbation is not a new
independent item. V12's missing-source variants were logically resolvable but
three demanded answers outside the frozen Noul/Score criteria; two corrections
also made the superseded source unnecessary. V13's pilot uses complete,
native-valid source **substitutions** as its decision-changing perturbation.
Do not generate uncertainty through deletion or redaction for Noul or Score.
Choice may include an explicit HOLD option when the contract defines evidence
insufficiency; that outcome must be specified in the unchanged rule and the
native criteria. A separate abstention stress set may later test missing
evidence but cannot silently enter the native three-type score.

## Candidate limits and authoring

Author at most 18 fresh independent originals, at most six per Choice, Noul
and Score. Require at least 12 distinct semantic operations and at least six
realistic evidence forms. No v9–v12 fact pack, operation instance, entity or
prose may be reused or lightly paraphrased. Every case defines a complete
decision contract before its evidence is written: entity universe, source
scope, temporal priority, missing-fact treatment, tie-breaks, units, ordered
Score bands and output vocabulary where relevant. The invariant contract is
identical in its original and counterfactual. For Noul, the proposition has a
Boolean answer under the stated facts. For Score, every answer belongs to the
declared ordinal criteria. The direct typed answer must be recoverable without
an unstated convention or a hidden source.

Each original uses at least two separately presented sources and at least two
resolution steps. Both sources must be materially necessary: hold one source
fixed and enumerate two domain-valid alternative contents of the other that
lead to different native-valid answers. Include these alternative-completion
witnesses in the private casebook. Then create one same-format, comparable
length **source substitution** per original that changes a decision-bearing
fact and changes the correct answer while leaving all answer options and the
rule valid. A separate oracle must derive both answers from structured facts;
hand-entered target labels cannot serve as the oracle. Reject a corrected or
superseded record if the earlier full record is no longer necessary for the
answer. Do not pad length: every paragraph must change scope, evidence,
precedence or a plausible competing interpretation.

Rotate semantic mechanisms, evidence order, answer class, option positions,
names and display form. Do not let a source title, field name, count, document
length, option position or one evidence field solve the answer without the
intended reasoning. Inspect repeated eight-word spans, source-body similarity,
cross-case structural clones and answer-only shallow heuristics. Source
substitutions, option permutations and label renamings are paired robustness
views, never extra independent originals.

## Frozen gates before any model run

1. Freeze the prospective rule/casebook, independent oracle, rendered original
   and substituted prompts, targets, native adapter/scorer, tokenizer revision
   and hashes. In the private casebook record rights/source provenance,
   author, decision proof and the two-source necessity witnesses. Keep private
   raw cases, keys, salts and joins off public source and gist.
2. Verify type-valid targets and criteria for every original and
   substitution, answer change, source necessity, complete contract, no
   silent truncation, and original/substitution lexical diversity. A single
   failing case blocks the frozen packet; repairs require a new version. Run
   exact and bounded near-overlap screens against rights-clean TRAIN/SELECT/
   CAL, all available authored/Score/multilingual rosters and exposed public
   and DEV questions. A text screen cannot prove semantic independence.
3. Reviewer A receives only salted gold-free originals. Reviewer B receives
   only independently salted, shuffled gold-free substitutions, without
   parent ID, intervention type, original answer, join, proof or target. B
   must never see A's packet or review before sealing. Both directly solve
   every row, record ambiguity, native-output mismatch, plausibility,
   unnecessary sources and suspected shortcuts, then seal row-level judgments
   with hashes and UTC times. Neither reviewer sees private targets first.
4. Check packet/manifest/review/seal hashes and chronology **before** opening
   the private key. Compare the two independent judgments with the frozen
   oracle, then publish only aggregate findings. Any material ambiguity,
   wrong join, native-output mismatch, false source necessity, formulaic
   filler or shortcut is a HOLD for the entire DEV version. A passed pilot
   only licenses planning a separately frozen 1,200–1,500-original pool, not
   publishing or training on these pilot items.

The eventual release pool requires a source-family and domain balance plan,
independent editorial/adjudication capacity, rights and semantic-overlap
audits, clustered uncertainty, and a separate pretest model/formula freeze.
Its 1,200–1,500 count is a quality target, not a quota to satisfy by
templates or paired variants. No historical public-set answer is rebranded as
a new authored item.
