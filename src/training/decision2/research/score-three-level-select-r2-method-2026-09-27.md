## Allocation construction and shortcut gate

For each group, choose two fixed resource pools with capacity, committed
amount and reserve floor held constant across the triplet. Define the raw
margin as `capacity - committed - request - reserve_floor`. Select one
strictly negative margin and one nonnegative margin for each pool. Across
all three levels, **each pool must have exactly those two raw margin values
and only those two margin signs**, not a negative/zero/positive ladder.
Use the same two request values for that pool whenever it appears in a
variant. Build the counterfactual ladder as follows:

| Level | Pool that changes first | Other pool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | negative | negative |
| 1 | nonnegative | negative |
| 2 | nonnegative | nonnegative |

Counterbalance which displayed pool changes first in exactly ten of the
20 allocation groups. Split that orientation 8/8 in English and 2/2 in
Chinese. Across groups, use both equality and positive slack as feasible
values, but keep each group's selected feasible value fixed. The native
rule counts the two feasible pools; equality remains feasible. Before any
row is admitted, assert that the three labels cannot be reconstructed from
one pool's entire record, raw margin, margin sign, feasibility bit, any
single numeric field, or its displayed position. In particular, no
single-pool projection may have three distinct values within a group.
An independently implemented oracle must agree on all 240 rows.

## Recheck the other three operations

For waiver precedence, freeze current veto and all unrelated fields within
a group. Vary only the waiver validity predicate for the blocked variant
and the secondary check for the highest variant. Every atomic waiver field,
the complete waiver subrecord, current veto field, and secondary field must
take at most two values across 0/1/2. A single waiver field or secondary
field cannot classify the whole triplet; the compound rule must be needed.
Keep the inclusive review-day boundary and current-versus-archived order
explicit.

For inclusive coverage, each current authority has one fixed covering
window and one fixed non-covering window within a group. Both endpoints of
the requested window are inclusive. Level 0 has neither covering, level 1
one covering, and level 2 both covering. Counterbalance which authority
changes first in an 8/8 English and 2/2 Chinese split. Assert each
authority's full window, start/end margin,
coverage bit and display position has at most two within-group values;
the archived window is fixed and nonoperative.

For independent quorum, keep the same report identifiers, lineages,
current flags, affirmative flags, order and **exactly three signatures**
across a group. Include at least three nonqualifying decoys and three
potentially qualifying reports, of which two share a lineage and another
has a different lineage. For level 0, sign three decoys; for level 1, sign
the two same-lineage qualifying reports and one decoy; for level 2, sign
two different-lineage qualifying reports and one decoy. Thus the raw
qualifying-report count is 0/2/2 while the distinct-lineage answer is
0/1/2; counting signed documents cannot solve the triplet.
Assert that each single report's full record, signed/current/affirmative
fields, document position and lineage has at most two within-group values.
The overall signed count and origin multiplicity must stay fixed. A
single report cannot solve the triplet; deduplication across reports is
required.

For every operation, enumerate these atomic and one-subrecord projections
as a frozen per-group shortcut suite. A feature uniquely identifying all
three answers in any group blocks the entire r2 freeze. Report
group-held-out shallow one-field accuracy for the whole 80-group panel;
any single-field feature above two-thirds on its operation is a blocker.
The independent oracle and a second derivation from rendered text must
agree on all rows. Keep all three variants together for overlap and
uncertainty. This gate does not claim to exclude semantic shortcuts that
were not anticipated here; the blind editor remains decisive.
