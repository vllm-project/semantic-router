## Calibration transport diagnostic

Positive temperature cannot change the option argmax. We mathematically
reconstructed the uncalibrated probabilities from the frozen calibrated
predictions using `p_uncal ∝ p_cal ** temperature`, recomputing Score's
probability-weighted mean before rescoring. This is a derived **diagnostic**,
not a second native inference run or a candidate-selection method. The first
derivation omitted that Score mean recomputation and made many rows invalid;
it is retained only as a failed diagnostic. The corrected v2 derivation has
zero invalid rows and the same 1,213/1,600 hard decisions. Corrected derived
report SHA-256 is
`ac83a8c228fca4a6a924587e9c48aa50d329186dcb2e01ac55a158a306708a9c`.

On independent typed DEV, normalized Brier **worsens** .17663→.19595 and
NLL .59556→.80887 after CAL temperature fitting. Score alone worsens Brier
.45295→.53404, NLL 1.60112→2.46994 and ECE10 .45026→.52111. The same
Score CAL90 improves ECE10 .18027→.08806. This is direct evidence that the
easy, narrow CAL distribution does not transport to the harder Score family.
Future runs need a separately frozen, varied hard Score CAL with at least
set, evidence and state-update tasks, while retaining no final-label use.

No sealed JevArena FINAL gold or authored release targets were used to train,
choose or calibrate this candidate. It is not publication-qualified: six-axis
JevArena cannot be computed until the authored panel passes blind review and
all same-panel baselines and parity gates are complete.
