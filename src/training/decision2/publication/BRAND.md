# Decision 2.0 visual direction

The provisional family mascot is an original, cute pixel-mosaic owl perched
where one branch becomes two. The fork stands for a decision; the owl's wide
eyes stand for checking evidence before choosing. Its rounded, die-cut outline
keeps the mosaic silhouette legible at Hugging Face collection and model-card
thumbnail sizes. The asset is `decision-2-sticker.png`.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Decision 2.0 / primary cobalt | `#315bff` |
| Open baseline / warm coral | `#f46b50` |
| Decision 1.0 / slate | `#738299` |
| Accent / golden yellow | `#f4b642` |
| Paper / warm cream | `#fff9ef` |
| Text / dark ink | `#172542` |

The palette is shared by rank, parameter-count Pareto and score-matrix SVGs.
Use the mascot as a small visual anchor alongside the title, not over a data
plot. Every chart keeps a textual title, direct labels, a plain-language scope
caveat and alternative text; color alone never encodes rank or frontier status.
The mascot is provisional until the card and collection are reviewed together.
