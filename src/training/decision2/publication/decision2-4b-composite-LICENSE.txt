Modification notice

DEV2.0-4B was fine-tuned from the pinned Eikos-4B weights for typed decisions
using a separate Decision 2.0 training mix, selected using a separate
selection set, and calibrated using a separate calibration set. The model
weights, calibration and accompanying inference files differ from the
upstream Eikos package. The source authors have not endorsed this derivative.
