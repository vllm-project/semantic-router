## 1. Banner

The banner is 2400 × 800 px on white, built from these elements:

- The codename in Inter Display Bold, filled with a `#0A5BD8` → `#5CC8FF` gradient, as the focal point.
- The size in ink Inter Display Bold on the codename's baseline. The baseline is set so that descenders (the "g" in
  "Vega") clear the tagline.
- The "DECISION 2.0" eyebrow in deep blue, and the small vLLM-SR logo top-left.
- The tagline "Structured decisions in one forward pass" in muted grey, ending in a yellow full stop.
- The logo's V-mark: only the yellow and blue shapes, cropped exactly to them, at 20 % opacity, bleeding off the
  right and bottom edges.

Inter (OFL) is passed to the renderer as before, and the receipt pins every font file by SHA-256.
`tests/test_card_banner.py` renders all six sizes. It checks that the logo, eyebrow, codename, size, tagline and dot
do not overlap, stay on the canvas, end at least 8 % of the width left of the V-mark, and that the codename is the
largest element. The test skips where matplotlib, the fonts (`DEV2_CARD_FONTS`) or the logo are missing.
