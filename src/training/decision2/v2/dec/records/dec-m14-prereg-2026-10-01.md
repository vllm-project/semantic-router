## Question

M12's additive breadth (IB1-r3 + IB2 on each tier's released recipe) gave large transfer gains (0.8B +.190, 2B +.151,
4B `LHA10` +.045) but each arm lost one typed head or family floor, and the loss moved between heads. In M12 the IB
rows carry a large share of every update: the trainer averages the per-row loss within each 64-row update window, so
a row's share of the gradient is its share of the window's rows, and IB rows are short and many (0.8B 47%, 2B 38%, 4B
19% of the rows). M14 asks whether **giving the released (typed) rows more loss weight than the IB rows** keeps the
typed floors while keeping the breadth. M13 tests the other lever (a teacher on the typed rows) and a smaller IB dose.

## Weights

- **Mechanism:** `train_dec --example-weights <file>` (added in this milestone, with tests): a JSONL of `{id, weight}`
  covering every TRAIN id exactly once (positive, finite), applied as per-example loss weights **normalized within each
  update window**, exactly as the trainer's existing `--type-balance` weights (loss = Σ wᵢℓᵢ / Σ wᵢ over the window).
  The TRAIN bytes, the windows, the update count and the LR schedule are therefore identical to M12's arm for the same
  seed; only the relative weight of released vs IB rows (and, at 2B, Score vs other released rows) changes. An
  all-released window is unchanged. Disclosed: this is not duplication (M13's `08b-RAAG` duplicates rows) and does
  not add updates or compute.
- **Released rows** are M12's `base` block (`train.ids.jsonl`): the tier's released TRAIN, byte for byte, as the first
  N lines (0.8B 162,696, 2B 56,141, 4B 58,739). `ops/m14/m14_weights.py` checks that the `base` block is exactly the
  first N lines and hashes to the released TRAIN (`f9f3c022…`, `1527b38b…`, `c385406e…`), then writes the weights in
  TRAIN order. Every IB row (and every `~c<k>` copy at 0.8B) has weight 1.
- **Resulting shares of the total loss weight** (from M12's row counts; IB rows are Choice / Noul only):

  | Arm | Released share (M12 → M14) | IB share | Score share (M12 → M14; released recipe alone) |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `08b-RAUP` | .526 → **.625** | .474 → .375 | .070 → .083 (.134) |
  | `2b-RAUP` | .616 → **.721** | .384 → .279 | .140 → **.203** (.227) |
  | `4b-LHA10UP` | .806 → **.862** | .194 → .138 | .196 → .209 (.243) |
