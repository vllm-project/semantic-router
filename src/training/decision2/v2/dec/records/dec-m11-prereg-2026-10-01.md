## Question

M10 found that a rank-128 LoRA from Qwen3.5-4B-Base with the scoring head (LH), on the released 4B mixture at
matched tokens, beats the released full fine-tune of Nox (post-key v3 +4.19 [+0.10, +9.88]) and keeps the base's
knowledge / maths (retention macro .770 vs .710; base .762). M11 asks whether the same recipe transfers to 2B and
0.8B, whose released models are full fine-tunes of Sol 1.0 / Eos 1.0.

### Training

`v2.dec.train_dec`, one epoch, token batching ≤ 32,768 tokens / ≤ 64 rows, 64 rows per update, warm-up .05 then cosine,
AdamW wd .01, gradient checkpointing, `even8` checkpoints with SELECT700 `matrix-v1` selection per seed (NT reads
SELECT700 through the label-token readout), seeds **20260926 / 20260927 / 20260928** (S2T's / E8F's). NT backbone
LR: 2B 5e-6 (S2T), 0.8B 1e-5 (E8F). Each seed runs zero-step, one-step and the `preflight_dec` gate first; a failed
preflight stops the arm (no rerun, no replacement seed). Arm artifact: the uniform FP32 soup of the seeds' BEST
checkpoints (LoRA seeds merged into full FP32 first, `m10_merge.py`); ≥ 2 finished seeds needed (two disclosed).

- Image `dbe5f32b`, one GPU per seed, each container given only its GPU's render node plus `/dev/kfd`
  (`ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES` remapped), `--network none`.
- **Autotune caches and pre-warm (new convention).** Per node and tier, two M11 Triton caches, each a `cp -a` copy of
  that node's M10 decoder cache (node B's seed + M10's entries): `<tier>-train` for training jobs and `<tier>-read` for
  readouts, so readouts never fill a cache that training fills. On each (node, tier) the first seed's preflight
  (zero-step, one-step, gate) runs **alone** on `<tier>-train`; the other seeds of that tier on that node wait for its
  pre-warm marker before they start (`m11-chains.sh`). Readouts on a GPU are serialized by a per-GPU lock.

### Development gates (per tier, against `<t>-C0` read on the same node; `ops/m11/m11_rules.py`)

A point passes when:

1. **M8 eligibility** (`ops/m8/m8_rules.py`): every type keeps c_t ≥ c_t,C0 − 0.03·n_t on typed DEV; no typed-DEV
   family below F_f,C0 − 0.10; Noul `rule_precedence` ≥ C0 − 0.01·n; HT-DEV v2 not FLAG (Δ > −0.02).
2. **Score5-typed-DEV floor:** 2B — the check half without COLLAPSE, and without WARN unless C0's has WARN (the 2B
   Score head watch: M8s found 2B Score the binding floor). 0.8B — C0's own check half is COLLAPSE (M8s amendment 3),
   so `m8s_rules.score_floor` applies: a point fails on a new level concentration (top share ≥ .90, or its Wilson upper
   ≥ .90 while C0's is below).
3. **Retention:** probe macro (MMLU, ARC, GSM8K) Δ vs C0, paired 95% CI upper bound ≥ 0.
4. **Yes-bias guard:** `hs1-dev` false-yes rate on unmet conditions (`hs1_unmet_condition`, Noul, gold no) not above
   C0's by more than 0.10.
5. **0.8B only (conservative; 0.8B development gains have not carried to formal before):** HT-DEV v2 Δ ≥ 0 (point
   estimate) — a development-only typed gain never qualifies a 0.8B point.

**Finalists (≤ 2 per tier):** passing points with an HT-DEV v2 GAIN (Δ ≥ +0.02) first, then the larger retention-macro
Δ. Diagnostics (never selected on): `hs1-dev` adoption, `pn1-dev`, typed T / H3 / proxy, the contrasts.
