## Question

M12–M16 added IB1-r3 + IB2 **on top of** LH's recipe (more tokens). Every such 4B point cost formal mlx-diag (M13
`4b-LHA10SD` −.029; M16 `-a50` / `-a75` −.015 / −.019), even after interpolation toward LH. The 9B track's K-a13IB
**swapped** IB into its released mixture at a constant token count (IB share 16.8%), passed formal item 4 (mlx-diag
−.004, n.s.) and was released on the Index path. M17 asks whether the swap carries to 4B: LH's recipe at LH's exact
token count, with English released rows replaced by IB rows, every non-English row kept, and typed-row
self-distillation from LH.

Differences from earlier swaps: M11 stage 2 (`4b-LHB`, a 25% swap that failed the Noul / Score type floors) cut every
family of the released mixture (non-English included) by 25%, used own-Lux KL on the kept rows and had no
self-distillation. 9B K-a13IB cut its whole x60 mixture (every language) and kept own-Lux KL. M17 cuts English rows
only, at 10% / 17% of T, with LH as the teacher on every kept released row.
