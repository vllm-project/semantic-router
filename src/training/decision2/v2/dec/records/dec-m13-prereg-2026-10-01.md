## Question

In M12, additive breadth (IB1-r3 + IB2 on top of each tier's released recipe) gave large transfer gains (4B +.05,
2B +.15, 0.8B +.19) with no HT-DEV v2 cost, but every point lost one typed head or family floor vs its release
(`08b-RA`: only the `attribute_gate` family floor; `2b-RA`: the Score type floor; `4b-LHA10`: Score; `4b-LHA`: choice /
Noul and retention). M13 asks whether protecting the typed heads keeps those floors while keeping the breadth:
**self-distillation from the released model on its own typed TRAIN rows**, or (0.8B) **upweighting the rows of the
failed family's skill**, or (4B) **a smaller IB dose**.

## Self-distillation (SD) targets (`ops/m13/m13-teach.sh`, `v2.dec.teacher_label`)

- **Typed rows = every row of the tier's released TRAIN file** (0.8B `m6-e8f-r2clean` 162,696 rows; 2B
  `m4-v2m-ret-r2` 56,141; 4B `m10-4b-base` 58,739). These are the rows that carry the released typed heads; every one
  is a Choice, Noul or Score row. **IB rows (and their copies) get no teacher: gold only** (`--teacher-partial`).
- Teacher = the tier's released model, loaded exactly as its readouts load it (DEV2.0 packages with their 1.0
  `--source-path`; LH soup with the 4B base). The release packages carry `calibration: null`, so the teacher's own soft
  targets are its **T = 1** distribution (`teacher_label --teacher-kind dec --uncalibrated`, added in this milestone;
  `--teacher-source-path` added so the teacher loads with its readout source).
- Loss on a typed row: gold CE + 0.5·Brier **plus** 1.0·KL(teacher ‖ student) (the trainer's teacher term is additive;
  disclosed: gold is not removed from typed rows). KL weight 1.0 at every tier (LH's own teacher weight). At 2B and 4B
  the SD targets **replace** the recipe's 1.0 teacher on those rows (2B own-Sol 0.5, 4B own-Lux 1.0): a row has one
  teacher distribution.
- Labeling: on the node that trains the arm (0.8B node E; 2B and 4B node F), one small pre-warm shard (rows at
  positions ≡ 0 mod 1000) alone on the tier's read cache, then one shard per GPU; joined targets must cover every
  released TRAIN id exactly once. The teacher's agreement with TRAIN gold per type is reported. Labels are hashed in
  the data lock.

## Data

- `08b-RASD`, `2b-RASD`, `4b-LHA10SD`: hard links of M12's locked `08b-RA` / `2b-RA` / `4b-LHA10` TRAIN (hash-checked).
- `4b-LHA5`: M12's `m12_data.py`, `4b-LHA5=all:0.05`, seed 20261001 (M12's rule: whole groups, stratified by family).
- **`08b-RAAG` (`ops/m13/m13_data.py`).** `attribute_gate` is a typed DEV generator family; by the data rule, TRAIN
  programmatic families are mechanism-disjoint from the DEV families, so **TRAIN has no `attribute_gate` rows**. The
  arm therefore upweights a declared proxy: the released 0.8B Choice families whose task is a **conjunctive
  eligibility gate over attributes with an explicit no-eligible / reject outcome** — `scoped_sources`,
  `stage3_evidence_scope`, `stage4_replay_stage3_evidence_scope`, `stage4_scope`, `authorization`,
  `stage4_replay_authorization`, `stage4_replay_policy` (≈ 25k rows; chosen from family task descriptions before any
  M13 data or readout, not from DEV items). **Weight 3**: M12's `08b-RA` TRAIN whole, then copies 2 and 3 of each of
  those released rows (id suffix `~a<k>`). Disclosed: this tests whether upweighting the skill, not the DEV family,
  restores the floor.
- Both nodes build every TRAIN file; the data lock requires byte-identical builds, pins every TRAIN and SD target hash
  and writes `m13/data/READY-m13.json` (arms, per-arm teachers) after it is pushed. Every seed re-hashes its TRAIN and
  teacher against it.
