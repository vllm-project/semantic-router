## Question

The private Index evidence (10:55 note; numbers stay private) puts 4B's largest deficits in (a) knowledge / maths
retention and (b) decision families absent from our training. Open entrants near the frontier are mostly decoders
read through LM-head label-token logits in one pass, several of them adapters on an official base. DEV2.0-4B is a full
fine-tune of Decision 1.0 Nox with a candidate scoring head. M10 tests (a) now, at matched tokens on the released 4B
recipe, and adds the IB1 breadth block when it lands.

- **H1 (retention by initialisation):** a LoRA, or a low-LR full fine-tune, from Qwen3.5-4B-Base keeps more of the
  base's knowledge / maths than the released full fine-tune from Nox.
- **H2 (readout):** an LM-head label-token readout keeps more of it, and transfers at least as well to held-out
  decision families (HT-DEV v2), as the candidate head on the same backbone.

### Label-token readout (`v2/dec/label_token.py`; prompt `decision2-label-token-v1`)

- The shared segmented prompt with each option on its own line behind a one-token label, and the cue
  "Select the single option best supported by the context and instructions.\nAnswer with its label only.\nAnswer:\n".
- Labels: Noul `false` / `true` → `no` / `yes`; Score levels 0–9 → the digit; Choice → A..Z, then the two-letter
  capitals that the tokenizer keeps as one token, in lexical order, up to 255 (the alphabet's SHA-256 is bound in the
  checkpoint).
- Option logit i = h_last · E[label_i] with E the tied input embedding (= the LM head), FP32, one forward pass. The
  label ids are read from the prompt (each option's `candidate_positions` entry is its label token), so the shared
  collate, SELECT evaluation, preflight and benchmark adapter run unchanged; the loss is the recipe's on these logits.
- Serving: a native `label_token` path in the release runtime with a parity test against `infer_dec` (committed before
  any finalist is packaged), designed so stock vLLM generate mode serves it unchanged: prompt token ids,
  `max_tokens=1`, `allowed_token_ids` = the row's label ids, per-token logprobs renormalised over the labels.

### Development gates (per arm soup, against C0 on the M10 path)

1. **Typed floors:** every type c_t ≥ c_t,C0 − 0.03·n_t; every typed-DEV family F_f ≥ F_f,C0 − 0.10.
2. **Noul floor:** `rule_precedence` c ≥ c_C0 − 0.01·400.
3. **Score floor:** Score5-typed-DEV check half without COLLAPSE, and without WARN unless C0's check half WARNs.
4. **HT-DEV v2 vs C0:** GAIN or TIE (ΔH_dev2 > −0.02).
5. **Retention vs C0:** the probe macro (mean of MMLU, ARC, GSM8K accuracy) Δ with a paired bootstrap 95% CI whose
   upper bound is ≥ 0 (not significantly below C0).

**Finalists (≤ 2):** among passing arm soups, an HT-DEV v2 GAIN first, then the larger retention-macro Δ vs C0. The
newest COORDINATION notes are re-read before the pick is fixed. No passer → no formal run; M10 reports development
only.

## Recommendation rule (fixed now)

- **Readout:** recommend the label-token readout for new 4B work if LT passes the development gates and LT − LH is
  ≥ 0 on HT-DEV v2 (point estimate) and on the retention macro with its 95% CI above 0; "neutral" if both CIs span 0;
  "head" otherwise.
- **Initialisation:** recommend base-init (LoRA or low-LR full, whichever ranks higher by the same order) over the Nox
  lineage if that arm passes the gates and its retention Δ vs C0 has a CI above 0; otherwise keep the Nox lineage.
- **Other tiers:** 2B / 0.8B / 9B inherit the 4B verdict as a prior, adjusted by each tier's recorded failure mode
  (2B Score-head shift under human Score rows; 0.8B development gains that did not carry to formal; 9B paraphrase
  yes-bias); 9B has the largest base knowledge to keep and is the first candidate after 4B.
