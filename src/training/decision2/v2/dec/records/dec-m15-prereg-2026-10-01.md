## Part B — question

M12 / M13 added IB1-r3 + IB2 (≈ 99.5% English) on top of each tier's released recipe. That lowered the TRAIN
multilingual token share (non-`en` rows, including `hi-en`, `es-en`, `zh-hant`) from .414 to .377 (4B `4b-LHA10`),
.455 to .365 (2B `2b-RA`) and .433 to .355 (0.8B `08b-RA`), and the multilingual diagnostic fell (4B −.029, 0.8B
−.014) with no development gate seeing it. M15 asks whether **keeping the released multilingual token share** while
adding IB (and typed-row self-distillation) keeps breadth without the multilingual cost, and adds a **development
multilingual guard** to every IB arm's gates.

## Data (`ops/m15/m15_data.py`, CPU, seed 20261002)

- **IB.** `4b-LHA10SDML`, `2b-RASDML`, `08b-RASDML` keep their M12 arm's TRAIN file whole (byte for byte, file
  order). The 10% arms keep the M12 arm's base rows whole and a subset of its IB lines with IB tokens = ⌊0.10·T⌋
  (T = base tokens, M12's `share` definition): copy levels in order (originals, then `~c2`, then `~c3`), each level
  kept whole while it fits and the last one sampled by whole groups stratified by family (M11's `sample_groups`).
  `2b-RA10SDML` = a sample of `2b-RA`'s IB rows; `08b-RA10SDML` = the whole pool once plus a `~c2` sample (pool
  10.17M < 13.82M tokens).
- **Multilingual-preserving upsampling.** s = the released TRAIN's multilingual token share (per-row tokens from M12's
  `train.ids.jsonl`, the tier's own tokenizer). With T, ML = base tokens / their multilingual part and I, I_ml = the
  arm's IB tokens / their multilingual part, the arm adds **U = (s·(T+I) − ML − I_ml) / (1 − s)** tokens of copies of
  released multilingual rows, so the TRAIN multilingual share equals s. Units are **whole groups whose rows are all
  non-`en`**, stratified by language in proportion to the language's multilingual base tokens (per language, groups
  in seeded order, a group is added while it fits its quota). A copy is its row's line with id suffix **`~m2`** (one
  copy per chosen group; U < ML at every tier). Required: the built share is within ±.002 of s; otherwise the build
  fails. Expected U from M12's counts: 4B ≈ 2.05M tokens (+17% of ML), 2B ≈ 6.01M (+45%), 0.8B ≈ 23.0M (+38%).
- **SD targets.** M13's per-tier `teacher-sd.jsonl` (every released TRAIN id once) plus, for every `~m2` copy, its
  original's target line under the copy id (the arm's own `teacher-ml.jsonl`). IB rows have no target.
- Both nodes build every arm from their own M12 / M13 files; the data lock requires byte-identical builds, pins every
  TRAIN and teacher hash and writes `m15/data/READY-m15.json`; every seed re-hashes against it.
