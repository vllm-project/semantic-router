## Release (Index-first; progressive; single Eos / Sol publisher after b49d1f36's in-flight releases land)

- **Gate:** paired-bootstrap Index 95% lower bound > 0 vs the tier's current release (its IX1 run on the released
  weights). Among candidates ready at the same time: the largest lower bound. A later candidate must beat the then-
  current release.
- **Integrity checks:** exact package parity (release.sh), Hub / `trust_remote_code` smoke under Transformers 5.17 /
  5.18, the row-level Index contamination audit of the candidate's TRAIN files (interpolations: their parents' audited
  TRAIN files), and the formal typed-FINAL panel with no collapsed type (one collection of the chosen candidate only).
- **References** (v3, human transfer, mlx-diag, public 231, C1) are not run as gates.
- Publish with b49d1f36's `release_ixf.sh` / `make_ixf.py` pattern, the default card generator (banner A), purge of
  the superseded weights, private; re-check `main` first; never concurrently with another publisher; integration
  (≥ `cd565a588`) merged first.
