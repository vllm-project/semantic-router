# Decoder track: 2B / 4B scale-up plan and Milestone 2 proposal

Status: **proposal**, not a preregistration. Each arm below gets its own frozen
preregistration before launch.

## What Milestone 1 established

1. **The development proxy does not predict post-key v3 at 2B/4B and cannot
   resolve factors at 0.8B.** At 4B, +3.63 on the development proxy became −0.48
   on v3 (E1-X2); the earlier own-Sol continuation went +2.19 → −1.6. At 0.8B the
   typed DEV panel sits at floor (Noul at chance, Score one constant level for
   every arm) and a data-order change alone moves the proxy by 1.19 (σ_seed 0.84).
2. **Rights-clean v2 continuation trades typed reasoning for human transfer.**
   E1-X2 raised CSS15 H (+.017; 11/15 tasks) but lost 37 exception-stack Noul
   answers on typed FINAL, the same family the earlier official-4B arm lost.
   Retention of the 1.0 model's typed reasoning is the binding problem at 4B.
3. **Own-Lux soft targets** are a confirmed development factor only at 4B (two
   seeds, +1.01); negative at 2B and 0.8B. **Wave-1 data arms** at 0.8B learn
   their own held-out families (+46 to +129 items) without transfer.
4. **Operations:** one job per GPU or a shared persistent Triton autotune cache
   is required (4–6 concurrent jobs on one GPU ran ≈5× slower); candidates must
   be collected on the comparator's node (0.64% of answers differ across nodes).

## GPU needs

Measured costs: 0.8B LoRA ≈3.9 s/update exclusive (466 updates ≈0.5 GPU-h), 2B
≈4 s (≈0.55 GPU-h), 4B ≈5.5 s (≈0.75 GPU-h); template-S arms ×1.3–1.7; formal
collection ≈0.15 GPU-h. Milestone 2 ≈ **18 GPU-hours**. With the current
allocation (node A GPU5 + node B GPU0–2) that is ≈5 h wall-clock with one job per
GPU. **Request:** keep those four GPUs; two more (for example node A GPU2–4 once
the 9B track releases them) would run the 0.8B breadth arms beside the 2B/4B
retention arms and cut wall-clock to ≈3 h.
