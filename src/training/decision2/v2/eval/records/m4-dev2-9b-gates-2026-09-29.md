**Candidate.** K-a13 = ⅓ × K soup + ⅔ × Lux 1.0 (FP32 weight interpolation, CAL698 per-type temperatures, 16,384
tokens, 7,940,895,744 loaded parameters). The K soup is the uniform soup of three seeds of Lux 1.0 full fine-tuning on

`m4/K-a13-build/soup` + `m4/K-a13-cal` (manifest `6913eb61836a`). The release name follows the loaded count (for

(T .8106 / H .5660); mlx-diag run `K-a13-16k-mlx`.

`7643d1aff` on node A's CPU. The 9B spec [`m4-dev2-9b-gates/overlap-spec-9b.json`](m4-dev2-9b-gates/overlap-spec-9b.json)

flagged item id).

## Verdict and card disclosures (9B, K-a13)

**All gates pass:**

- K-a13 beats the adopted Lux 1.0 with a lower bound of +0.61.
- Human transfer is not significantly below Lux 1.0 (the tier's best measured model) or Nimble v2.
- No type collapsed, for K-a13 or for Lux 1.0.
- There is no overlap exposure, and removing the flagged items changes no conclusion.

The card must disclose:

- **The gain over Lux 1.0 is typed reasoning** (T +0.034 [+0.015, +0.054]). Human transfer is level (+0.008
  [−0.011, +0.042]). Show Lux 1.0 at its stricter adopted run (65.81) and note the same-renderer control (65.23).
- **Nimble v2 comparison.** The v3 margin is significant (+5.68 [+3.19, +10.09]); the H margin is not (+0.030
  [−0.007, +0.102]).
- **Typed Choice is below Nimble v2:** .920 vs .980.
- **Score** uses all five levels. Its level-0 recall is below Lux 1.0's (.51 vs .69, 35 items), and level 4 is
  under-predicted (104 predicted vs 128 gold).
- **JevBench public 231** (a public-subset reproduction): 178 vs 183 for Lux 1.0, within noise. The hard tier is 64 vs
  68, and long public inputs are 19 vs 22 of 37.
- **Multilingual (mlx-diag, non-English Choice / Noul).**
  - Non-English Choice is level with Lux 1.0 (79.4% vs 78.3%).
  - Non-English Noul is 2.5 points lower (80.8% vs 83.3%, significant). The weakest languages are Korean (70% vs 77%)
    and Spanish (84% vs 88%), 100 items each.
- **CSS15 regressions vs Lux 1.0:** `talklife` −.022, `wiki_corpus` −.021, `tropes` −.011, `conv_go_awry` −.001.
  CSS15 long inputs are level.
- **Invalid answers:** 4 CSS15 answers, all inputs over the 16,384-token limit (as for Lux 1.0), counted as failures.
- **Calibration.** The formal run used the CAL698 temperatures.
  - Typed ECE is .048 against Lux 1.0's .017 (Brier .113 vs .119); CSS15 median ECE is .079 vs .136.
  - Adoption follows the 23:15 rule, decided by release engineering on the development panels. Answers do not depend
    on temperature. If T = 1 ships, the card lists the rejected temperatures.
- **Weight origin.** ⅔ own Lux 1.0 (`Decision-1.0-Lux-9B@bd45a30a`) plus ⅓ K soup: three seeds of Lux 1.0 full
  fine-tuning on 60.2M native tokens, then FP32 weight interpolation. Release engineering states Lux 1.0's upstream
  lineage and licence.
- **Teacher.** Own Lux 1.0 soft targets on every training row, including the H7 / H8 rows (wave h-w1). They were
  produced on the node-B Lux runtime image, which lacks the causal-conv1d kernel.
- **Training data.** The XL r2 subsample x60: 122,651 rows in 35 languages, English 55% of rows. It includes the human
  arms (A7q OASST1 ratings, H1, H8) and H7 long evidence. Credit the data licences.
