## Corrections to the request (details in prereg §2)

- The typed panels come from `benchmark/generate.py`. `publication/generate_arena_v3.py` and
  `scripts/generate_postkey_artifacts_v3.py` render card artifacts.
- **Typed FINAL Score is one family, `resource_ledger`** (5 levels, 100 groups × 4 variants), not four. Typed FINAL has
  four families, but the other three ask Choice/Noul. "FINAL's family mix" is therefore 100% `resource_ledger`. This also
  corrects the M7 record §3 line and the 14:05 cross-track note.
- The family is tiny: 90 answer-relevant structures, of which FINAL covers 80, and 360 states once event ids are
  ignored, of which FINAL covers 152. Text n-gram tools see nothing in these items, so near-duplicates are defined
  structurally: the same ledger up to event ids and row order.

## Reading and limitations

- **What it measures:** level usage on typed FINAL's own Score family, drawn fresh. That is why it catches the collapse
  that the in-family A7q panel (Score5-DEV) and the 3-level typed-DEV Score missed. It says nothing about other 5-level
  Score formats (human-rated, multilingual, long); keep reading CSS / HT-DEV / Score5-DEV for those.
- **Same family as FINAL by design.** About 75% of groups share an answer structure with FINAL. A correction fitted on the
  fit half is therefore fitted on FINAL's Score distribution; the card must say so, as the 14:05 note requires.
- **Validation scope:** six 0.6B-tier models from one 2.0 lineage plus Kai1. Other tiers and lineages are untested.
- **Near the line:** the 0.90 collapse line is hard. m7-mxcx sits at .876 on the full panel and gets WARN on the halves.
- **Reproducibility:** public seeds make the panel reproducible from the repo. It is a development panel, not sealed.
