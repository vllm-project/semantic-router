## 3. Rules (registered)

**Matching (G0).** These are the data track's Index-row guard rules (`v2.data.ib1.index_guard`, reused, not
re-implemented): NFKC, casefold, `\w+` tokens.

- **E:** raw equality of a leaf of at least 20 characters.
- **N1:** equality of a whole normalized leaf of at least 6 tokens.
- **N2:** equality of a normalized sentence, or a whole leaf, of at least 8 tokens.
- **G:** any shared word 13-gram within one leaf.

The leaves compared:

- **Item side:** every string leaf of the item's state, its overlap texts and its question (instructions and the
  option or level texts).
- **Training side:** every string leaf of the row's state (JSON strings are decoded), its instructions and its
  option descriptions.

**Boilerplate** (declared now; reported apart and never quarantining):

- **B1, training templates.** An instructions or option text that occurs verbatim in at least 50 rows of its
  family in the same file. These are the builders' fixed strings; G0 reports these apart as well.
- **B2, C1 templates.** An item unit (any rule) that occurs in at least 50 items of the item's own task. These are
  the task's fixed instructions and option or level texts; the threshold is IX1's template filter. No other
  exemption exists: stock phrases outside B1 / B2 count.

**The registered scanner.** It gives OVERLAP for an exact match of at least 8 tokens or containment ≥ 0.5, and
REVIEW for 0.2–0.5 or a shorter exact match. It runs as at events 2–3 and has no boilerplate exemption.

**Decision per file.** A scored v1.2 item is **exposed** to a file when either of these holds:

- a row of the file has a G0 hit on it outside B1 / B2;
- the registered scanner gives it OVERLAP or REVIEW against that file.

| Outcome | Rule | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PASS** | No exposed item (boilerplate-only hits are disclosed) | Nothing to do |
| **QUARANTINE** | At least one exposed item | Every group of every row that links to an exposed item goes on a private list, published by count and SHA-256. Future mixtures must leave those groups out |

Hits on the 34 retired items are disclosed and do not count.

**This is the P2 rule applied to these files.** Each file holds training rows of candidate models, which is P2's
class (a). There P2 allows no OVERLAP or REVIEW hit on a row still in the item set. Here "in the item set" is decided
exactly, item by item, instead of through the source rows. P2's template-free scanner is kept, and only G0's
template units are exempt.

**Arms and item 8.**

- An arm's exposure is the number of scored items exposed to any file it trained on, counting only rows in
  families the arm kept. For an arm that drew a sample of a file, this is an upper bound.
- The spec maps 28 arms across 27B M5 / M6, 9B M7 / M9 and decoder M7 / M11–M14.
- **Item 8 is valid only for an arm whose exposure is 0.** An arm with exposure > 0 gets no item-8 run on its
  current weights. Its track either retrains without the quarantined groups (then rechecks) or takes it to the
  coordinator. C1's item set stays v1.2: no retirement after the key, since the item-8 baselines are pinned to it.

**Integrity checks.** Each failure stops the run without a verdict:

- **Planted controls.** 200 seeded scored items have a state leaf with a non-template 13-gram. Each such leaf is
  planted as a synthetic training row, once exactly and once perturbed (as in G0's controls). All 200 exact copies,
  and all 200 perturbed copies through N2 or G, must be flagged outside the boilerplate. The controls are built in
  memory and never written.
- **The item mapping** must give 2,874 items, of which 2,840 are scored.
- **Hash bindings.** The registered scanner's receipt must name the hash of the mapped items, and every training
  file must match its pin.

**Disclosed only.** The protected-row scan (all 67,617 source rows) reports counts of non-CLEAN rows by C1 source and
file. It is a superset view of P2's own unit, the source row; most of those rows feed no selected item.
