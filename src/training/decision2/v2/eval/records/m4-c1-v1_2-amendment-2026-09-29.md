## 1. Item set v1.2

**v1.2 = v1.1 (2,874 items) minus the items of every retired candidate.** The retire rule:

- **(a)** every item that contains, or derives from, the `narrative_gold` passage matching the raw NQ extract behind H7,
  in all four annotation files;
- **(b)** every ID newly non-CLEAN since event 2 (the 13);
- **(c)** all 17 REVIEW IDs;
- **(d)** any other item that shares a source passage with (a)–(c).

**How the rule is applied** (`v2/eval/sealed/retire.py`, code `a64652c24`):

1. **Flagged rows.** The 21 protected rows the failed scan marked non-CLEAN (hits `75056c48…`): 4 OVERLAP and
   17 REVIEW, which include the 13 new IDs. The four OVERLAP rows are the one passage in the four annotation files.
2. **Why candidates, not items.** Protected rows are raw rows of the C1 source snapshots. C1 items are a salted
   selection of the build candidates, and the salt stays encrypted. So the rule retires candidates; an item is dropped
   when its candidate is retired.
3. **Links.** A build candidate (build-5 pools, all nine files hash-checked) is linked to a flagged row in either of
   two ways:
   - **by id:** an `id` or `*_id` field of the raw row, re-read from its snapshot and checked against the protected
     row, names the candidate's source group or source item;
   - **by text:** one side holds at least half of the other's shingles, the scanner's own OVERLAP standard.
4. **Closure.** Every candidate in the C1 source group of a linked candidate is retired, since a group is one source
   passage: the `narrative_gold` passage, the `legal_case_law` case, the `delichess` dialogue, the `tutormoments`
   transcript, the `implicaturex` item, the `hallutruthqa` question.

**Result** (receipt `c98a158d…`, count-only):

- **Retired list:** private, on node A at `private/sealed/c1/v1_2/RETIRED-v1_2.json`. SHA-256
  **`bbf095c70917f028d691fce570b114725e4f22fd6a1987456f6ae2c30f22990a`**. It retires **555 candidates** and records

- **Rows with no candidate:** three flagged rows derive none. They are the `hallutruthqa` train row (the converter

The salt is sealed, so only a bound is known before decryption. For each task: at most
min(selected, the sum over retired groups of min(group cap, retired candidates the build kept)). The event reports
the realized count.

- **Items.** At most 48 of 2,874 items are retired (1.7%), so v1.2 keeps at least **2,826 items** in all 14 tasks.
  Every task keeps at least 186 items, so no task is emptied.
- **Precision.** C1 is the mean of per-task macro-F1. Its standard error grows by a factor of at most **1.0075** at
  equal item variance, and at most **1.063** in the worst case (the legal task alone). A paired 95% interval of
  ±2.50 points therefore widens to about ±2.52, and to at most ±2.66. The minimum detectable paired difference rises in
  the same proportion (under 1% typically).

## 3. What is scored

- **Collection is unchanged.** Every model answers all 2,874 prompts, and predictions are sealed over all of them
  before gold is decrypted.
- **Scoring uses v1.2.** `score` and `compare` take the pinned retired list (`--retired`/`--retired-sha`) and drop
  the retired candidates' gold rows. Every `REPORT-C1.json` and `PAIRED-C1-*.json` records an `item_set` block:
  version, list hash, dropped and scored items, dropped by task.
- **Every event-3 pairing is on v1.2.** That includes Kai 1.0 at 8K against the stored DEV2.0-0.6B event-2
  predictions, and the Kai 1.0 8K vs 1,024 reference pair. The stored predictions' seals are re-checked before the key,
  and they are re-scored on v1.2 into the event directory (`event3.sh`, `event3 summary`).
- **v1.1 results stand as recorded.** Events 1 and 2 are not rescored.

## 4. The rescan (coverage fix, `d992e8d70`)

The v1.1 containment argument missed raw source extracts and trained pools. The rescan now covers by rule, not by a
hand-built list (`c1-rescan-coverage.json`, `coverage.py`, `c1-rescan.sh`).

**Scope on each node.** Every text-bearing file under the program's data roots, whatever its age:

- `/data/dev2/{runs,private,tmp}`;
- the Hugging Face caches' dataset snapshots and the content-addressed blob store;
- the prior-session roots `/data/decision20-*`, `/data/decision2-*`, `/data/jev-research-*` and
  `/root/decision2-private`.

That covers the raw extracts behind every arm and every pool of every arm, released or not. It also covers everything
landed since event 2 up to the scan, including round-1 data if present: 27B M4 / M4b, 9B M5, decoder M6 and the data
track's paraphrase arm.

**Excluded:** code mirrors, the sealed set, eval panels, eval outputs and dev panels (`runs/eval`, `private/eval`,
`private/htdev`), the eval-artifacts snapshot, leases and logs, autotune caches, and model-metadata files.

**How files are read:**

- archives are extracted and compressed files decompressed;
- `.txt` and `.md` files are scanned line by line;
- suffix-less blobs are sniffed by content (JSON, text and parquet in, weights out);
- files modified in the last 90 minutes are copied first, so a writer can't change them mid-scan.

**Nodes.**

- **Node A** scans its files in place, plus every version of every private training-data file landed after the
  event-2 revision `30e0a1f7` (`hf-delta`).
- **Node B** scans its own files in place, with the same image (`f83b1d10`), parameters (`--workers 48
  --exact-min-tokens 8`) and protected rows. For that scan the protected rows (`36797f50…`) are copied to node B's
  `private/sealed/c1-rescan/` (mode 700). They are all splits of the public C1 sources and carry no salt, so they
  identify no C1 item. The copy is hash-checked, logged in both nodes' `ACCESS.log`, and deleted after the scan.
- Both containers run with `--network none`, no GPU device, and the sealed directory hidden except the protected rows.

**PASS rule** (`scanverdict judge`, merged over the nodes per protected row, worst verdict and highest containment):
among protected rows **not** in the v1.2 retired list,

- no OVERLAP;
- no row non-CLEAN now that was CLEAN at event 2 (baseline: the event-2 recheck hits `077f1b53…`);
- no recurring row with higher containment than at event 2.

The 21 retired rows are exempt. `event3.sh` reads the key only after `scanverdict check-v2` accepts that PASS verdict,
pinned by its SHA-256, for this retired list and these protected rows.

**If the rescan fails only on flagged rows** (not on coverage or integrity):

- the same rule runs once more as **v1.3**: `retire.py --previous` on the rescan's merged non-CLEAN rows;
- v1.3 is committed and pushed before its rescan;
- at most two amendment iterations in total;
- if the v1.3 rescan also fails, it is recorded, the event stops, and event 3 stays unused.
