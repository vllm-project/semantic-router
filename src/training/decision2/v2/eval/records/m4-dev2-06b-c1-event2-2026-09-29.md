## 4. Reading

- **The candidate clearly beats its own 1.0 on the independent set.** It leads Kai by +15.44 [+13.51, +17.21],
  and every type's interval is above 0. It also leads Lex by +12.39 [+10.30, +14.29]. On post-key v3 the margin over
  Kai was +7.60.
- **Part of that margin is Kai's input limit.** 525 of the 2,874 items (18%) exceed Kai's published 1,024-token
  limit and count as wrong, as they do in the card's v3 table. On the 11 tasks whose inputs fit that limit, the mean
  task macro-F1 is still 29.4 vs 20.3.
  - That figure is a point estimate from the reports. It has no interval, because a restricted bootstrap would need
    the gold again.
  - A same-limit Kai1 run on C1 (8,192 tokens, `dev2-06b-kai-native-8k`) was not collected; it was not in the
    confirmed batch.
- **It is level with Bosun v3.1 0.6B**: −1.82 [−4.05, +0.35]. Bosun is significantly better on Choice and Noul, and
  the candidate is significantly better on Score. This is the same pattern as the v3 gate disclosure (typed accuracy
  below Bosun).
- **It is clearly above GLiNER2.5-Decide**: +10.39 [+8.20, +12.55], where v3 had them level. GLiNER2.5 cannot take
  935 items, and on Score the two are level.
- **Noul is the weakest type**, at 47.6, with all three Noul tasks below 50. This matches the typed disclosure that
  Noul is only narrowly above chance.
- **Score is the strength relative to the 0.6B peers.** On Russian star ratings its QWK is .51, against .00 for Kai
  and .01 for Bosun.
