# Multilingual diagnostic panel MLX-diag v1 and first results

Eval & peers track, Milestone 2 item c. **Development diagnostic, not a release score**
and not a sealed test: the sources are public test splits. Builder, isolation audit
and scorer: `python3 -m v2.eval.multilingual_panel {build,audit,score}`; the panel is
registered as `mlx-diag` in `v2/eval/panels.py`.

## Panel

| Type | Source (publisher TEST split only) | Languages | Source items × languages | Construction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choice | MASSIVE 1.1 (CC BY 4.0), archive `4cba5faa…`, per-locale files pinned | en ar de es fr ja zh | 126 × 7 = 882 | scenario of a voice-assistant request, 18 fixed options (7 per scenario); the earlier precommitted localisation filter (≥2/3 intent judgments, median grammar ≥3, target language) must pass in all six non-English locales |
| Noul | PAWS-X `4cd8187c` (free use, attribution) | en de es fr ja ko zh | 100 × 7 = 700 | "same meaning?" over the aligned sentence pair; 50/50 labels |
| Score | XNLI `b8dd5d7a` (**CC BY-NC 4.0**, internal use only) | en ar de es fr ru zh | 99 × 7 = 693 | three ordered support levels (contradicts < neither < supports), one hypothesis per premise, balanced |

2,275 prompts, SHA-256 `25fde28f4b227a9cc8588da79a597ce816ba4a8f979139f99f69aeb595580a5f`;
gold `71515a41583e7c4792c7058d45e6b12980f033bc9de2847b15dd3fb7b940b484`. Rows are chosen
by a salted hash of the source ID (plus label balance), never by model output.
Instructions and options are English and the state is in the target language, so this
measures cross-lingual reading under English-instructed native questions.

**Isolation.** None of these test splits appears in any project record. The research &
data track's arms use KLUE and JGLUE TRAIN splits only and exclude the MASSIVE lineage
(its A5 is ko/ja). An exact and 8-gram (16-character for short text) shingle audit against
all TRAIN, AHO and replay files of dataset revision `f8c50b13`, every formal and dev
panel, and prior MASSIVE-derived and multilingual dev data flagged 12 source items
(9 short MASSIVE commands, 3 PAWS-X Wikipedia sentences); those items were excluded in
every language and the quotas refilled from the next hashed IDs. The final audit
flags **0**. Training tracks must not use MASSIVE/PAWS-X/XNLI test splits.

Accuracies in %; chance is 5.6% (Choice), 50% (Noul), 33% (Score). Cross-language
agreement is the share of source items answered identically in all seven languages; a
near-constant model (Lex) scores high on it, so read it together with accuracy.

Findings: every model loses 3–7 points off English; Korean (PAWS-X) or Arabic is the
weakest language for most models; the 0.6B encoders are far behind (Kai and Lex below
Bosun 0.6B), and Lex is close to chance on Choice. Weaknesses of the panel: public test
splits may be in backbone pretraining; XNLI is translated from English and its
three-way labels are only a proxy for ordinal Score; PAWS-X Noul has known lexical-
overlap shortcuts; Choice uses a fixed 18-way label set with English descriptions.

GPU time: 1,286 GPU-seconds (0.357 GPU-hour) for the 14 runs on node A.
