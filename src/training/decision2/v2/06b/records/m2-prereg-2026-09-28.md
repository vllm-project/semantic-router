## Architecture change being tested (QB)

Start: official `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-Base@da87bfb6` (Apache-2.0), 596,049,920 backbone
parameters plus the shared `CandidateHead` (597.1M loaded; counted at export).
Change against the archived official-Qwen causal control: the decoder attends
**bidirectionally** (a prepared 4D key-padding mask replaces the causal mask and
the attention modules' causal flag is cleared) over Kai's candidate-marker layout
`[BOS] {type} question [SEP] ([MARK] candidate [SEP])... state [SEP]`; every
candidate marker is scored against the BOS state by the same shared head. Recipe,
data, 466-update budget and loss (CE + 0.5 Brier, LR 2e-5/2e-4, 5% warmup) match
the causal control and EB610/MB307. The zero-step check must additionally show
that the state (placed after the markers) changes candidate scores, which a causal
mask would forbid.

## Own-Lux1 teacher (TRAIN prompts only)

`Decision-1.0-Lux-9B@bd45a30a` through `inference.run --backend lux
--over-budget-invalid`, on node A GPU0–1 only, with a private copy of the eval
track's frozen Triton autotune cache (`TRITON_CACHE_AUTOTUNING=1`,
`HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=1`); cache tree hashes before and after are recorded.
Inputs are the 7,455 rights-clean TRAIN rows as System One requests; no panel item
is ever sent. Screen on TRAIN gold (the own-Kai thresholds as fractions): all rows
valid; Choice and Noul agreement ≥ .625; Score ≥ .375; three-level Score ≥ .375;
each type's mean half-Brier ≤ .25. A failed screen stops QBL and LXL.
