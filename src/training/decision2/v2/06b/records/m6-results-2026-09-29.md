## 4. What was learned

1. **30M-token XL r2 families with own-Lux KL on every row lift typed accuracy strongly.**
   - Seed-mean Q is 41.1 / 41.4, against 33.4–36.6 for the M4/M5 families.
   - Formal T is .512, against .395 for the released model, .415 for Z and .435 for X. Typed FINAL
     Choice is 389 against 255.
   - Formal human transfer slips (H .459 against .480, not significant). Per-task CSS15 deltas are
     in §1: the losses are concentrated in reddit humor and the two wiki tasks.
2. **Data v2 no longer costs development transfer at the XL budget.** The m6-mx soup has H3 .400
   against m6-cx's .387; the M4 V2 soup had .335. SemEval stance holds (.527; the V2 soup had .312).
3. **Score readout collapses toward the top level.**
   - The m6-cx family is near-constant on typed DEV: s2 at 100% level 2, the soup at 98.5%.
   - Finalist 1 is 94.75% level 4 on typed FINAL.
   - A plausible cause is own-Lux KL on the human Score rows: arm (a) trained them on gold and
     used three levels. This is not attributed; the recipes differ in other ways too.
   - Adding arm (a) (gold human Score) de-collapsed it (78.7%), at a v3 cost (−1.62).
   - Typed FINAL Score accuracy is flat for every formal 0.6B soup (131–148/400), close to the
     majority-level 128.
4. **Neither version of the development guard predicted the formal collapse.** `m6-mxcx-soup`'s
   typed DEV modal share was .83 with three levels.
   - Typed DEV Score items offer levels 0–2, and typed FINAL offers 0–4.
   - A guard on typed DEV level shares cannot see a collapse onto FINAL's top level.
5. **Greedy:** after m6-cx, no older group helped. Adding a, V2 or T each lowered T_dev and Q.
