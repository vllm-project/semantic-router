## 0. Successor rule (formal run only)

Every formal run uses the eval track's frozen runner on node A: image `f83b1d10`, the 8,192-token package,
and a fresh copy of the frozen autotune cache (tree `e215f8bd…`). A candidate is a successor only if all
three conditions hold:

- **S1 (v3):** the paired v3 Δ against the released `m4-t-a7-soup` (its M4 formal run, identical to the M6
  control) has a 95% CI lower bound > 0 (5,000 draws).
- **S2 (human transfer):** the ΔH interval against the released model is not entirely below 0, i.e. its
  upper bound is ≥ 0.
- **S3 (no type collapsed):**
  1. `v2.eval.gates types` marks no type COLLAPSED (Choice, Noul and Score).
  2. On typed FINAL Score, no single answer value covers ≥ 90% of the 400 answers.
  3. Typed FINAL Score accuracy is clearly above always-majority. Operationally, accuracy minus the accuracy
     of always answering the most frequent gold level of those 400 items has a paired bootstrap 95% lower
     bound > 0 (5,000 item resamples, seed `20260929`). Wilson intervals are reported alongside.

Each formal run also reports paired Δ against Kai1, Kai1 8K, Bosun, GLiNER2.5-Decide, Z, X, Lex, the causal
control and `m6-mxcx-soup`, plus public 231 (E/S/H) and mlx-diag.

If both (a) and (b) produce successors, both are reported. The recommendation is the (b) successor unless
(a) is significantly better on v3 (paired Δ lower bound > 0 between them). The (b) successor needs no
runtime change, and it fixes the cause rather than the symptom.

## 1. (a) Per-level Score bias correction of the frozen `m6-mxcx-soup`

### 1.1 The correction

- For a Score question with L levels (keys `"0"…"L-1"` in level order) and model logits z_0…z_{L−1},
  the corrected logits are z_k + b_{L,k}.
- Temperature (T = 1) and softmax are then applied as before, and the reported `score` (expected level) is
  recomputed from the corrected probabilities.
- Only L ∈ {3, 4, 5} is corrected: these are the Score level counts of the development and formal panels
  (typed DEV 3, mlx-diag 3, public 231 4 and 5, typed FINAL 5).
- Every other L, every Choice and Noul answer, and all weights stay unchanged.
- The offsets live in a `score_bias.json` bound to the model's weight fingerprint (like the `--calibration`
  report) and are applied by `training.model.infer --score-bias` (a small shared-module change with tests,
  in its own commit).

### 1.2 Fit and check data

**FIT** = the CAL698 Score rows (90 rows, all 5-level; `m2/cal/CAL698/cal.jsonl`, sha256 `19cc1a8c…`) ∪
the FIT half of AHO.

**AHO** = the held-out diagnostic slices (split `select`) of the six arms in the m6-mx / m6-cx training
recipes that contain 5-level Score rows: A7q, H6, G6, A7g, A6g and A6h. Only their Score rows with
L ∈ {3, 4, 5} are used. Node A paths are recorded in the M7a report.

Before splitting, a row is dropped if any of these hold:

- its id, input sha256 or group id appears in any of the six M6 training mixtures, in SELECT, or in
  CAL700/CAL698;
- its group is among the r2 rescreen's excluded groups;
- its state text exactly matches an item of a formal or development panel (typed FINAL / DEV, CSS15 / CSS
  pilot, public 231, mlx-diag, HT-DEV).

C1 is sealed; these arms passed the C1 independence rechecks.

**Split:** a row goes to FIT if `int(sha256("m7a-aho-split:" + group)[:16], 16)` is even, and to CHK
otherwise. `group` is the row's group id, or its id if it has none, so a group never straddles the split.

**Never used for fitting:** typed FINAL, v3, public 231, mlx-diag, the CSS pilot / CSS15, HT-DEV, C1, and
typed DEV. Typed DEV is used for checking only.

### 1.3 Logits

- FIT and CHK logits are the log-probabilities from the frozen export under the trainer's evaluation path
  (`train.evaluate`, FP32 at T = 1). This is the path that wrote the soup's `cal.probs.jsonl`, and the CAL698
  Score rows reuse that file (joined by id).
- The formal adapter runs BF16 autocast with an FP32 head. Check A-D3 below verifies that adapter, bias file
  included.

### 1.4 Fit

- For each L ∈ {3, 4, 5} with at least 150 FIT rows, minimize the class-balanced NLL
  Σ_k (1/n_k) Σ_{i ∈ FIT_L, y_i = k} −log softmax(z_i + b)_{y_i}, with b_{L,0} = 0.
- The solver is Newton's method in float64 until the gradient norm is < 1e-9. The offsets are then shifted
  to mean zero (softmax-invariant) and rounded to 6 decimals.
- The rounded file values are what every check and the formal run apply.
- An L with fewer than 150 FIT rows keeps zero offsets. An L whose FIT set misses a level stops the fit, and
  the failure is recorded.
- **Why class-balanced:** it estimates the model's own level prior on held-out in-family data and replaces
  it with a uniform prior. It never uses the prior of any evaluation panel.

### 1.5 Development check (all must hold before the formal run)

- **A-D1 (CHK, 5 levels):**
  - the corrected modal level share is ≤ .90;
  - corrected accuracy minus always-majority accuracy (majority gold level of CHK_5) has a paired
    bootstrap 95% lower bound > 0 (5,000 draws, seed `20260929`).
- **A-D2 (typed-DEV Score, 400 items, 3 levels):** the offsets are applied to the logged probabilities of
  the soup's typed-DEV readout.
  - the corrected modal share is ≤ .90;
  - corrected accuracy is ≥ 101/400, the M6 guard's Score floor (the released model's typed-DEV Score).
- **A-D3 (implementation):** a typed-DEV collection with the formal adapter plus the bias file must
  reproduce the offline-corrected answers:
  - the same argmax on every Score item;
  - identical Choice and Noul answers;
  - max |Δp| ≤ 1e-4 (BF16 vs logged BF16).
- **Reported, not gated:**
  - on FIT, CHK_3, CHK_4, CHK_5 and each arm: level usage, accuracy, always-majority accuracy, Cohen's κ
    and per-level recall, before and after the correction;
  - typed-DEV Score confusion before and after;
  - paired Δ accuracy against the uncorrected soup.

If any check fails, (a) gets no formal run, and the failure is recorded.

### 1.6 Formal run (at most one)

- **Package:** the `m6-mxcx-soup` export files (byte-identical) plus `score_bias.json`, with its own
  manifest. The revision is that manifest's sha256.
- **Adapter:** `dev2-06b-causal-8k` plus `--score-bias`.
- **Runner:** the M6 formal script generalized to an M7 root (same comparators and the same frozen cache
  snapshot), plus the S3 Score gate.
- The formal run may be a recorded co-tenant on one of the track's GPUs while (b) trains. M6 showed that
  the model path does no autotuning and that co-tenancy leaves answers and the cache unchanged. Latencies
  from a co-tenant run are not comparable.

## 2. (b) Root-cause fix: gold labels on the human-rated Score rows

### 2.1 Families

- **m7-mx-s{1,2,3} and m7-cx-s{1,2,3}** match m6-mx-s{k} / m6-cx-s{k} exactly in:
  - the materialized mixture files (same sha256, same rows, same order);
  - the seed strings `20260928-06b-m6-s{k}` (hence the same data order);
  - the start, head seed, optimizer, losses (CE 1.0, Brier 0.5, teacher KL 1.0), eighths milestones, BEST
    rule (SELECT, step 0 excluded), collapse stop (SELECT < 350 at or after 3/8) and 1.8 GPU-hour cap.
- **The only change is the teacher file.**
  - Every row of the pools A7q, A7k, A7s, H6 and V1:A6h is removed from the M6 own-Lux teacher file. The
    kept lines stay byte-identical.
  - The removed rows train on their gold labels only (CE 1.0 + Brier 0.5, no KL). Every other row keeps its
    M6 own-Lux target.
  - The arm spec's teacher `coverage` becomes `"subset"`.
- **H6 disclosure:** H6 also contains derived multi-hop evidence-coverage Score families (about 46% of its
  rows). The brief names H6 as a whole, so all H6 rows are gold-only.
- **Expected build:**
  - teacher rows: cx 100,079 of 141,261; mx 137,450 of 188,882;
  - teacher-covered rows per seed: cx 37,698 / 37,693 / 37,782; mx 50,226 / 50,299 / 50,019.
  - The build must match these counts exactly, and `m6_partition verify --teacher` must show missing
    targets only in those five pools. Otherwise stop and record.
- **Why the M6 seed strings:** they make the pairs m7-x-sk vs m6-x-sk matched, so the development contrast
  isolates own-Lux targets on the human Score rows.

### 2.2 Execution

- As M6: one `train.py --preflight` per family (on s1). A failed preflight stops that family.
- Queue: GPU0 runs m7-mx-s1 → s2 → s3, and GPU1 runs m7-cx-s1 → s2 → s3. Each seed gets its typed-DEV +
  CSS-pilot readout.
- A collapsed or failed seed is recorded and not rerun.

### 2.3 Soups and readouts

- **Soups:** m7-mx-soup and m7-cx-soup (uniform over each family's COMPLETE seeds), and the cross soup
  m7-mxcx-soup (all COMPLETE seeds of both families, uniform), as in M6.
  - A family with only 2 COMPLETE seeds soups those two, disclosed. With fewer than 2 the family is
    dropped.
- **Readouts:** typed DEV + CSS pilot (Q = 100·√(T_dev·H3) with H3 the CSS-pilot three-task mean, P, and the
  M6 amended guard). They also cover typed-DEV Score level usage and accuracy, and CHK from §1.2
  (uncorrected, trainer path).
- **Family artifact:** the soup if its Q ≥ the family's seed-mean Q, else the median seed (17:15 rule).
- **Matched contrasts (development evidence, not gated):** m7-x-sk − m6-x-sk per seed and per soup (ΔP /
  ΔQ with 10,000 draws, typed-DEV Score, CHK_5 accuracy and level usage).

### 2.4 Finalists (at most two formal runs)

- **Eligible:** a soup or family artifact that passes all of:
  - the M6 amended guard: typed-DEV Choice ≥ 255, Score ≥ 101, modal Score share ≤ .90, SELECT and CAL
    Noul ≥ .85;
  - B-D1: A-D1 applied to its uncorrected CHK_5 answers.
- **Order:**
  1. m7-mxcx-soup, the direct analogue of the M6 finalist, if eligible;
  2. then the highest-Q other eligible candidate, provided it is within 8 Q of the best eligible
     candidate (proxy v2 band).
- No eligible candidate means no formal run for (b).
- Together with (a) this stays within the proxy v2 cap of three finalists per tier per milestone.
