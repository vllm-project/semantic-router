## 1. Part 1: soup and constant typed-DEV Noul

- **(a2) soup** (Milestone 3 seeds, different head seeds): P 28.53 against a seed mean of
  28.83. That is a tie, formally below the mean. It misses the typed Choice floor (137).
  Averaging two differently initialised heads did not break the model.
- **Typed-DEV Noul is a capability gap, not a bug.** The slot is one family,
  `rule_precedence`. On it, every Milestone 2–3 0.6B checkpoint has AUC .44–.53, yet
  p(true) moves toward the gold within 68–93 of 100 counterfactual pairs. A CAL-fitted
  bias moves the slot by −1 to +10 answers, so none was adopted. Every model up to 4B
  scores .39–.56 on it. No A0s or A7 row has its facts-plus-rules form. Details are in
  `m4b-prereg` §0 and `m4/noul-diag.json`. Mixture v2 gave the first signal: the V2 soup
  reaches .60 on `rule_precedence`, and V2 s1 has AUC .589.

## 5. Decisions and deviations

- **Taken under the autonomy mandate:**
  - A7 v3 instead of v2, because v3 passed the embedding scan.
  - A0s renumbered in the builder with the shared tool, pending the data track's
    renumbered A0s.
  - Mixture v2 adopted as a third arm, and the control trimmed to two seeds (M4c).
  - V2 moved to the scaled own-Lux targets as soon as they were published (M4d).
  - The typed-DEV Noul floor replaced by an in-distribution Noul check after the T soup's
    readout (M4e). It was written before the V2 readouts and any formal run.
  - T soup's formal run started before V2 s3 finished. Only a V2 s3 P ≥ 42.7 could have
    removed T under the tie rule; it came in at 35.67.
- **Not run:** Kai1 continuation (low prior); a hard-label V2 control.
- **Failures:** none of the arms failed. The only non-zero exit is the intentionally
  blocked control seed 3 (M4c).
