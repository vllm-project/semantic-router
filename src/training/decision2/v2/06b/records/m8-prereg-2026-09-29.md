## 1. The correction

- Additive offsets b_0…b_4 on the logits of **5-level Score questions only** (keys `"0"…"4"`), before T = 1 and
  softmax; the expected `score` is recomputed. Applied by `training.model.infer --score-bias` (`8730d9413`), file
  format `dev2-score-bias-v1` bound to `model_sha256` `01fae750…`, with `offsets` holding the key `"5"` only.
- Every other level count, every Choice and Noul answer and all weights are unchanged. On the formal panels only
  the 400 typed FINAL Score items (`resource_ledger`, one quarter of axis T) and 1 public-231 item are 5-level
  Score. CSS15 (axis H) has no Score item, mlx-diag Score is 3-level and typed-DEV Score is 3-level.

## 3. Candidates (six fits, CPU only)

For source s ∈ {ledger, human} with rows R_s and gold counts n_{s,k}, row i gets the within-source weight
a_i = n_{s,y_i}^(−λ) / Σ_{j∈R_s} n_{s,y_j}^(−λ). The fit minimizes

J(b) = Σ_{i∈R_ledger} a_i ℓ_i(b) + w_h Σ_{i∈R_human} a_i ℓ_i(b), with ℓ_i(b) = −log softmax(z_i + b)_{y_i} and b_0 = 0,

by Newton's method in float64 with backtracking until the gradient norm is < 1e-9 (at most 200 steps). The offsets
are shifted to mean zero and rounded to 6 decimals; the rounded file values are what every check and formal run
apply. λ = 1 is M7's class-balanced objective; λ = 0 is plain NLL (matching the fit half's level prior); λ = ½ lies
between. Rationale for the grid: the model has little ledger signal (full-panel gain +.009 [−.001, +.019]), so a
uniform-prior correction risks accuracy near chance (the collapse rule's Wilson ≤ .20 clause), and a prior-matching
correction risks keeping the top share high. The development gates decide.

| Candidate | λ | w_h | Order (tie-break) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `s5-b1` (core) | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| `s5h-b1` | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| `s5-b05` | ½ | 0 | 3 |
| `s5h-b05` | ½ | 1 | 4 |
| `s5-b0` | 0 | 0 | 5 |
| `s5h-b0` | 0 | 1 | 6 |

w_h = 1 gives the human block the same total weight as the ledger block. No second model arm: `m6-mxcxa-soup` is
+3.29 [−1.12, +7.74] against the released model, and offsets can move only the ledger family, so they cannot plausibly
lift its bound; the three formal slots go to offsets of the strongest checkpoint.

## 4. Development gates (all must hold)

- **D1 (score5t-dev check half, 400):** the readout's own computation (`v2.eval.score5t.blocks(...)["check"]`) on the
  offline-corrected answers flags neither COLLAPSE nor WARN: top share < .90, top-share Wilson upper bound < .90 and
  accuracy Wilson lower bound > .20.
- **D2 (typed-DEV Score, 400, 3 levels):** corrected top share ≤ .90 (the 5-level offsets leave it at .828;
  recomputed anyway).
- **D3 (CHK_5, 1,809; human 5-level held-out):** (a) paired Δ accuracy, corrected − uncorrected, has a 95% bootstrap
  upper bound ≥ 0 (5,000 item resamples, seed `20260929`), i.e. not significantly worse; (b) corrected modal share
  ≤ .90; (c) corrected accuracy − always-majority has a paired bootstrap lower bound > 0 (M7 A-D1).

**Reported, not gated:** on the score5t fit half, check half and full panel: histogram, top share with Wilson CI,
accuracy (Wilson and bootstrap), always-majority accuracy, accuracy − always-majority with its CI, NO-GAIN, macro-F1,
QWK; CHK_5 by arm; the Score5-DEV panel before and after; the offsets. Always-majority is reported next to every
accuracy.
