## 3. What was learned

- **Same-init recipe soups are the working lever at 0.6B.** X and Z needed no training beyond
  the arms and are the two best 0.6B models measured.
  - Souping V2's weights into T added typed reasoning, without the transfer loss V2 shows alone
    (X H .473 against V2 .397). X beats both ingredients on evidence join and resource ledger.
  - Adding arm (a) moved H to .498, the best 0.6B value, halved typed ECE, and lifted
    constraint competition (.280 from .235).
- **(a) A7 human multilingual Score (A7q/A7k/A7s at 20% of tokens):**
  - It is the only data arm with a positive transfer signal (pilot mean .427).
  - Typed DEV Score gets three levels and rises 101 → 165.
  - Typed DEV Choice is unchanged, and the single-factor rule is not met.
- **(b) Mixture v2 with Choice protected does not help at 0.6B:**
  - Typed DEV Choice rises (301).
  - SemEval stance collapses (.395 against T's .539), as with V2 (.312), and the arm fails the
    guard.
  - Keeping T's Choice rows does not remove v2's transfer cost. The v2 slice's extractive-QA
    answerability Noul is the prime suspect.
- **(c) AutoJev-27B targets are no better than own-Lux for this student** (paired soup Δ −0.04).
  They also fail the guard. Lux stays the clean default at 0.6B.
- **The pilot mean tracked human transfer.** Across the four formal soups (V2, X, T, Z) the pilot
  mean ordered CSS15 H correctly (.335 → .397; .386 → .473 and .480; .402 → .498). The median
  H_pilot did not.
- **P ranked the soups in v3 order** (Z > X > T). The calibration line under-predicts both
  finalists by 1.9–2.8, within its ±3.1 leave-one-out error.
- **Seed variance (σ_seed(P) 2.4–3.3)** again rivals the tie band. Per-seed contrasts flip sign
  within one arm.

## 4. Operations

- **One 0.6B training process saturates an MI325X** (100% use, ≈ 8K tokens/s). Three concurrent
  seeds per GPU gave the same aggregate throughput as sequential runs, so they only changed the
  schedule. Peak memory was ≤ 25.6 GiB per process, and the longest run took 2.03 h (cap 3.0).
- **Arm (c) seeds were split** across GPU0 (s1, s3) and GPU1 (s2) with `m5_arm.sh`'s per-seed
  GPUs (`62a4be02f`), to balance the two GPUs.

## 5. Decisions, deviations and failures

- **M5-1: X went to the formal runner before the arms (disclosed).** Its stated premise, that X
  could lose its slot only if two later candidates both beat it by P, was incomplete.
  - Under the pre-amendment §4 rule, the finalists would have been Z and A*: X and A* are within
    the tie band, and A* has the higher pilot mean.
  - Under M5-1 they are X and Z. **Z is a finalist under both readings.**
  - For the last slot (X excluded), Z ranks first by P (38.92 against A* 35.40), so no tie-break
    applies.
- **M5-2 (disclosed):** Z was redefined as T ⊕ V2 ⊕ every guard-passing arm. This was written
  after X's formal result and before any arm readout. Only arm (a) passed the guard.
  - Z's spec (`records/arms/m5-z-soup.json`) was written on node A by `m5_zspec.py`.
    `m5_zspec.py` is orchestration, committed after use; the soup itself ran from mirror
    `62a4be02f`.
- **Deviation, export runtime: the M5 soup steps ran `soup.py` without `DEV2_PYTHON=python3`.**
  They used the container's default Kai/Lex venv (Transformers 4.57.6), not the image Python
  (5.17.0).
  - **What it affected:** the five soup exports (X, a, b, c and Z) carry 4.57.6-format tokenizer
    and config files. The soup states, readouts and formal runs are unaffected, as shown below.
  - **Parity check:** X and Z were re-exported under 5.17.0 (`m5-x-soup-r517`, manifest
    `2cdad9df…`; `m5-z-soup-r517`, manifest `7c6e79e4…`).
    - The soup states were bit-identical.
    - On typed DEV 1,600 + CSS pilot 1,430, answers, token usage and input hashes were identical
      to the scored packages, as were P and typed and pilot results.
    - Only the package fingerprint differs.
  - **Staging:** the staged Z holds the exact scored bytes, with the equivalent 5.17 re-export
    recorded in `STAGING.json`.
  - **Fix:** future soups set `DEV2_PYTHON=python3`.
- **Failures:** no training arm failed.
