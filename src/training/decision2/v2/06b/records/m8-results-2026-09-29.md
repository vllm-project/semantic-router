## 7. What was learned

1. **A 5-level offset correction fixes the collapse without touching the rest of the model.** Typed FINAL Score top
   share goes .9475 → .620 and accuracy .3275 → .3425; nothing else moves. The panel's check half predicted FINAL's top
   share closely: .625 → .620 for `s5-b05`, .820 → .790 for `s5h-b05`.
2. **The model's ledger Score skill remains weak.** Accuracy stays within noise of always-majority on FINAL (+.0225
   [−.015, +.063]), and level 3 is never predicted. The correction fixes level usage, not skill.
3. **The objective mattered.** A uniform-prior correction (λ = 1) over-corrects and costs human 5-level accuracy
   (−.035). A prior-matching correction (λ = 0) keeps the collapse, because the fit half's majority level is the model's
   modal level. λ = ½ was the only setting that passed every gate.
4. **Adding human rows (w_h = 1) protected human Score** (CHK_5 +.006 against −.011), but left the ledger top share
   near the line (.82 check, .79 FINAL).
