## 1. Amendment M5-1: X goes to the formal runner now, as finalist 1

Part 1 §4 picks finalists after every candidate is read. Under the autonomy mandate X's formal
run starts now, before the arms. The reasons:

- X is at least 4 P above the released candidate and passes the floors and the guard.
- Under §4, X can lose its slot only if two later candidates both beat it by P.
- An early answer decides whether a successor exists now. If one does, the report is immediate.
- The early answer also tests whether the pilot guard protects v3 transfer. That tells whether
  V2-type data hurts CSS15 in ways the pilot cannot see.

**The second and last formal slot** goes to the best of A*, B*, C* and Z under the §4 rule, with X
excluded from that competition. It is used whatever X's outcome, because an arm may still give
a stronger successor. No third formal run.
