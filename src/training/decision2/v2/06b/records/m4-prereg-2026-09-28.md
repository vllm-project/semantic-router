## 2. Constant typed-DEV Noul answer

Measured before this prereg from the Milestone 2–3 readouts (CPU, aggregates only):
typed-DEV Noul is one family (`rule_precedence`: 400 questions, 208 true, one instruction
template, structured facts + prioritised rules state, 100 groups of four with
counterfactual / order / label pairs). Every causal checkpoint answers p(true) in
.17–.49 (always false); AUC of p(true) against gold is .44–.53 for all eight 0.6B causal
and Kai checkpoints read; even a threshold chosen on DEV gold itself reaches at most
217/400 (always-true gives 208). Across the eval track's 16-model matrix typed-DEV Noul
is .39–.56 for every model up to 4B and .65–.76 only at 9B–27B. TRAIN Noul is balanced
(A0s .50 true; A7 sub-arms .49–.51) and no A0s or A7 Noul row uses the facts + rules
state form.

Remaining step (`noul_diag.py`, reproducible): CAL probabilities of the four (a2)/(a2c)
BEST checkpoints (`soup.py`, one ingredient, no export), a Noul logit bias and a
threshold fitted on CAL only and applied to typed DEV, plus the panel and TRAIN
summaries above. **Rule:** a post-hoc Noul bias or threshold enters a candidate package
only if the checkpoints show ranking signal on typed-DEV Noul (AUC ≥ .60 on every seed)
and the CAL fit raises typed-DEV Noul without making the answer constant; with AUC ≈ .5
any threshold only swaps one constant answer for the other, and nothing is adopted.
