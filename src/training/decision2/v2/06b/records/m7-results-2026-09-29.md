## 4. What was learned

1. **The FINAL Score collapse is not caused by own-Lux targets on the human-rated Score rows.**
   - Training those rows on gold changed typed-DEV Score usage completely (modal .83 → .46) and raised
     held-out CHK_5 accuracy.
   - On typed FINAL both finalists still answer level 4 on 83–99% of Score items, at or below the
     always-majority accuracy.
2. **At matched data, own-Lux targets on those rows helped the formal score.**
   - Removing them costs −3.80 v3 for the cross soup (T .470 against .512; H .425 against .459).
   - The M6 finalist remains the strongest 0.6B by v3.
3. **A bias correction fitted only on permitted data cannot fix it.** In-family held-out Score is not
   top-biased, and the fitted correction pushes typed DEV further onto the top level.
4. **No permitted development set reproduces the FINAL collapse.**
   - `m7-mx-soup` passed every development guard: typed-DEV modal .45, CHK_5 modal .34, accuracy +.18 above
     majority. It then answered level 4 on 98.5% of typed FINAL Score.
   - Typed DEV covers one 3-level family. The 5-level held-out slices are healthy for every M6/M7 checkpoint.
   - The binding problem is measurement: Score fixes cannot be selected on development data until a 5-level
     development panel from the FINAL Score families' generators exists (fresh draws, never FINAL items).
