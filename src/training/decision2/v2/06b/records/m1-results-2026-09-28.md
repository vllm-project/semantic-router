## Findings

1. **Kai lineage.** Admitting all 7,455 rows at 8K (K8H) matches Kai2's
   in-distribution SELECT (523 versus 516) but still lowers transfer: CSS pilot
   H .1748 → .1556, Noul 203 → 192, with Choice up 137 → 175. Soft self-replay
   (K8S, 0.5·KL to Kai1's own distributions) slows SELECT learning (503) and
   does not protect transfer (H .1535). Neither beats unchanged Kai1.
2. **Lex is the better Kai-lineage start.** L8H improves over Lex1 on H
   (.1760 → .2016) and slightly on T, for P 26.01 → 27.97; the gain is not yet
   known to exceed seed noise (the M2a seed repeat measures it). Its typed-DEV
   Score output is still one constant level, as it is for Lex1.
3. **Every Kai-lineage, Qwen and encoder model predicts a single Score level on
   typed DEV** (three-level rubric). The data has only 102 three-level Score
   rows; this is a data coverage limit, not something these recipes fix.
4. **EuroBERT-610m collapsed** under the Qwen-control recipe: gradient norm fell
   from ~20 to ~0.04 around updates 110–125 with loss at chance, and CSS pilot
   predictions became degenerate. mmBERT-base under the identical recipe and
   head did not collapse, so the design is trainable; the failure is specific to
   this optimizer setting on EuroBERT (M2a tests a lower LR/longer warmup).
5. **Fresh official encoders are undertrained at this budget.** MB307 was still
   rising at its final update (410 → 453 over the last 175 updates) and ended
   below Kai1 on transfer. Seven thousand rows are not enough to build a decision
   head from scratch; Kai/Lex carry prior decision training.

## Incidents (none changed an arm's data, recipe or selection)

- The first encoder zero-step attempt used a synthetic ledger state longer than
  8,192 EuroBERT tokens; the packer correctly refused to truncate it. The fixture
  was shortened to the preregistered ~6K size and both checks were rerun.
- A CPU contract test auto-selected the image's Triton flash-attention on CPU
  tensors; the test now pins SDPA (the production loader already did).
- The first K8H and L8H readouts computed predictions but could not write them
  (missing output directory); nothing was scored. Readouts reran from the fixed
  commit `96eb10dfa`.
- An orchestration step aborted after the test failure and briefly skipped the
  encoder arms; they were re-queued behind their zero-step checks.
