## 2. Recipe and arms

(a2) unchanged: official Qwen3-0.6B-Base `da87bfb6` causal endpoints + global query head,
backbone LR 1e-5, head 2e-4, warmup 10%, cosine to 1e-6, batch 16, padded eight-row
micro-batches (32,768-token budget), CE + 0.5 Brier + 1.0 KL, one epoch, BEST by SELECT
family-macro at eighths. **All seeds share head seed 20260928** (same init, so their soup
is valid); seeds differ in data order (`20260928-06b-m4-s1|s2|s3`). Collapse stop: SELECT
< 350 at or after the 3/8 checkpoint (T update 614, C update 838). GPU-hour cap 1.6 per run.

## 3. Decisions fixed now

- **A7 effect** (T − C): per seed pair, the paired contrast of `contrast.py`; the effect
  counts only if the 95% lower bound of ΔP is > 0 on every seed pair and the seed-mean
  ΔP exceeds 2σ_seed (σ_seed = SD of P over the three seeds of the noisier arm).
- **Candidate** (17:15 rule): from T, the uniform soup of its three seeds (same init) if
  its P ≥ the T seed mean, otherwise the median-P seed. It must clear the floors (CSS
  pilot H ≥ .281, typed Choice ≥ 186/800, Noul ≥ 186/400) and predict at least the
  0.6B height bar (P ≥ 30.5, i.e. 19.12 + 0.629·P ≥ 38.3) to get a formal run. At most
  two formal candidates in the milestone; a second only if tied with the first
  (|ΔP| < 4) and from a different recipe.
- **Formal run** (frozen runner, node A, package at 8,192 tokens, adapter
  `dev2-06b-causal-8k`): post-key v3 + public 231 + `mlx-diag`, paired against Kai1,
  Kai1 at 8K, Lex, Bosun, GLiNER2.5-Decide and the causal-Qwen control. Release threshold:
  paired 95% lower bound vs Kai1 > 0 **and** v3 ≥ 38.3. A candidate meeting it is uploaded
  to a private HF staging model repo and reported at once.
- If T's seed mean P is not above C's by the effect rule, A7 (as the Eos/Lux pool at 20M
  tokens) is recorded as *not passed* at 0.6B; the candidate rule above still applies to T.
