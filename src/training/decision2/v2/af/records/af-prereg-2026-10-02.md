## Why

Cross-arm FP32 weight averages of diverse frozen arms from the same base were the day's strongest lever (4B
`SDMLxALL` above every single arm; 27B `M6-IB x IB2` above both parents). The owners (M17 4B, M10 9B) are in release
and soup phases. The factory trains more diverse, good arms on idle GPUs, builds their soups and BF16 release copies,
measures candidates once on the Index, and hands them to the owners. **The factory never publishes.**

## Placement

- 9B: node A GPU1–6 (GPU7 is M10's prerelease). 4B: node C GPU3–7 first; node F GPU2–7 and node B GPU2 / 4 / 6 / 7
  only once their current owners release them. Lease `track=arm-factory`, checked before each seed (a GPU held by
  another track is never taken); render-node isolation on every node (`ops/af-launch.sh`).
- Each node's first seed is its pre-warm seed (preflights alone on the node's Triton cache, a copy of the owner's warm
  cache); the others wait for its marker.
- Assets move node to node (relay through node A / B / C), never through the workstation, and are hash-checked.

## Preflights and stop rules

Per seed: zero-step, one-step, `preflight_dec` gate, the full run only on PASS (`ops/af-arm.sh`). A failed preflight
stops its arm (no rerun, no replacement seed). Seed caps: 4B 2.5 GPU-h, 9B 4.5 GPU-h. Node training gates (no seed
starts above): node A 20, node C 13, node F 6, node B 4 GPU-h. A failed or capped seed is not rerun; one finished seed
is the arm's artifact (disclosed).

## Candidates and measurement

- Each candidate is measured **once**, on its BF16 release copy, with the IX1 Index harness (the owners' paths: M17
  `m17-index.sh` / `m17_ixpool.py`, M10 `ix.sh`), sharded over free arm-factory GPUs. Base runs: 4B
  `IS-4b-LHA10SDML-bf16` (the current Nox weights), 9B `K-a13IB-bf16`; paired bootstrap as the owners run it.
- **Measured:** the new-recipe arm soups (`4b-LHS17IB4-lrh`, `4b-LHS17IB4ML`, `4b-LHS23IB4`; 9B `KIB4W2`, `KIB4L2` at
  a40) and the factory's cross-arm averages:
  - 4B `4b-AFxALL`: the uniform FP32 average of the better of M17's wave-5 member sets (`SDMLxALL9` or `SDMLxALL15`,
    by their Index runs, ties to fewer members) plus every factory 4B arm soup; one more weighting only if budget
    allows. M17's own `SDMLxALL9` / `SDMLxALL15` are not rebuilt.
  - 9B `KF`: the uniform FP32 soup of every finished KIB4-family seed (M10's KIB4 s1–s3 and the factory's KIB4
    s4–s5, KIB4W2, KIB4L2) at a40 and a50; then `KF × KIB` (K-a13IB's arm soup) at most two weights.
- The seed-extension soups (`4b-LHS17IB4`, `4b-SDMLIB4`, `4b-LHS17UP` new seeds; KIB4 s4–s5) are members, measured
  alone only if budget remains.
- Choosing among a few soups by their Index results is a mild selection on the test panel; the owners' gates (lower
  bound > 0 vs the then-current release, integrity checks, contamination audit of every member's TRAIN) decide
  release.
