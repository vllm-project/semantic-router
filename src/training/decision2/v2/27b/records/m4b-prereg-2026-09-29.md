## Questions

- **Q1 (capacity).** Does full-parameter fine-tuning of Qwen3.8-27B on F1's exact mixture, at matched tokens, beat
  F1's LoRA? The interest is mainly typed reasoning on the unseen families where the gap sits (constraint
  competition .570 vs 1.000, exception stack .788 vs .843).
- **Q2 (human-transfer lever).** With everything else identical (file, seed, order, updates), do own-Lux soft targets
  on the human-rated rows (gold kept) raise human transfer over gold only?
- **Q3 (interpolation).** Does θ(α) = α·S + (1 − α)·F1 keep S's typed gain while holding human transfer at F1's level?

## Trainer (new; `v2/27b/m4b/train_ff.py`, FSDP2 over three ranks)

- **Model:** the same class as F1: `training.model.decision_model.DecisionModel.from_base` on the pinned base
  (`source_stage` posttrained, `head_variant` shared, head dim 256), all 25,624,600,064 backbone parameters plus the
  5,263,872-parameter head trainable.
- **Head init:** a fresh `CandidateHead`, built by the same seeded construction path as `training.model.train`.
  Arm-seed k uses F1's seed k (s1 = 20260926, s2 = 20260928), so each FF seed starts from the head init of F1's seed
  with the same number. A CPU test checks that the two paths give bitwise-identical initial heads.
- **Sharding and precision:** `torch.distributed.fsdp.fully_shard` per decoder layer, the head as its own unit, then
  the root, via `torchrun --standalone --nproc-per-node 3`.
  - Parameters, gradients and AdamW states are sharded in FP32; compute is in BF16, with the head and loss in FP32 as
    in `training.model`, and gradient reduction is in FP32.
  - The BF16 mechanism is autocast with its weight cache disabled, or the FSDP mixed-precision policy, whichever
    passes preflight P0 (below). The choice is recorded by amendment before any 27B job.
  - Gradient checkpointing is on, and the image's FLA / causal-conv1d kernels are on the import path.
- **Objective:** `ce_brier` (CE + 0.5·Brier over the offered options) on gold for every row, plus
  w·KL(teacher ‖ student) on the soft-target rows only (`--teacher-partial` semantics of `v2/dec/train_dec.py`).
  The teacher probabilities are used as stored (T = 1).
- **Optimizer:** AdamW (β 0.9 / 0.999, eps 1e-8, weight decay .01), clip 1.0.
  - Backbone peak LR **1e-5**, head **1e-4**.
  - **10% linear warmup**, then cosine to the 0.1×-peak floor of `training.model`'s learning factor.
- **Batching:** the decoder trainer's token batching, with micro-batches of ≤ 32,768 padded tokens and ≤ 64 rows per
  rank.
  - Each update holds 64 rows (global), split deterministically across the ranks; the last update may be short.
  - One pass over the file, max length 4,096 (every row of F1's file is admitted at 4,096), about 404 updates.
  - The loss is the sum over the update's rows divided by its row count.
- **Selection:** SELECT700 `32a4352d…`, read in-process at `ceil(updates/8)`-spaced updates plus the final one (F1's
  cadence). BEST uses F1's rule: family-macro accuracy, then −Brier, then the earliest step.
- **Checkpoints:** a full FP32 checkpoint in the `DecisionModel` layout (`checkpoint_format: full`) is written
  whenever a new BEST appears and at the final update. The last full checkpoint that is not BEST may be deleted after
  the run.

## Candidate artifacts and the interpolation line (c)

- **Per arm with two seeds:** the exact uniform FP32 soup of the two BEST checkpoints (streamed per tensor, backbone
  and head; max |soup − mean| ≤ 1e-6 relative). The 17:15 rule picks the artifact: the soup if P_dev(soup) ≥ the
  mean of its seeds' P_dev, otherwise the seed with the higher SELECT700 score. A3's artifact is its seed's BEST.
- **F1M:** F1's LoRA soup merged into the pinned base in FP32 (`training.model.materialize`, PEFT safe merge), with
  F1's head.
  - Merge check: every merged projection equals base + s·BA in float64 within 1e-6 relative.
  - F1M's development readout must agree with F1's on ≥ 99% of typed-DEV + CSS-pilot argmax answers. Otherwise the
    line stops and the failure is recorded.
- **Line:** θ(α) = α·S + (1 − α)·F1M in FP32, streamed per tensor (backbone and head), for α ∈ {⅓, ½, ⅔}. S is A2's
  artifact, or A1's if A2 has none.
- **α rule (development only, never v3), with F1's readout as the reference:**
  - α is **eligible** if all three hold:
    1. every typed-DEV type's correct count is ≥ F1's − 0.03·n_t;
    2. H3 (the CSS-pilot three-task mean) ≥ F1's H3;
    3. no typed-DEV family is more than 0.10 below F1's.
  - Let G(α) = T_dev(α) − T_dev(F1) and G* the largest G over eligible α ∈ {⅓, ½, ⅔, 1} (α = 1 is S).
  - **α\*** = the smallest eligible α ∈ {⅓, ½, ⅔} with G(α) ≥ 0.75·G*.
  - The line has **no pick** if no α is eligible, G* < 0.01, or only α = 1 qualifies.

  - Node B GPU0–2, image `dbe5f32b`, 32,768 tokens, eval runner and 27B kernel adapter.

## Preflights and stop rules

- **P0, engineering (once, before any 27B training).** A failure stops the milestone's training until it is fixed by
  a new commit; P0 is then repeated. It is an engineering preflight, not an arm.
  1. CPU unit tests pass: rank split, loss normalization, teacher join, interpolation endpoints, save/reload.
  2. A tiny random Qwen3.5-architecture model (FLA path), 3-rank FSDP vs a 1-GPU reference in FP32: two updates with
     loss equal within 1e-5 relative, updated parameters within 1e-4 relative, and a bitwise save → reload.
  3. **27B probe on A1-s1's settings:** 24 updates, no checkpoint kept. It measures peak memory (must be ≤ 245 GB
     per GPU, else micro-batches drop to 16,384 tokens by amendment) and throughput.
  4. The probe's speed (not results) sets the per-attempt caps by amendment before the first full attempt.
- **Per arm-seed:**
  - admission: every row ≤ 4,096 (known for this file);
  - one-step: one update with a finite loss and gradient norm;
  - **reload:** save the one-step model, reload it through the inference path, and score 32 SELECT rows: 0 argmax
    changes and max |Δp| ≤ 1e-4 vs the trainer's in-process probabilities.
  - A failed preflight stops that arm-seed. It is recorded and not rerun.
- **Stop rules:** a nonfinite loss, an OOM or a cap breach stops the arm-seed.
  - At most one restart from scratch (same seed, code and file) is allowed after an infrastructure fault not caused
    by the arm's data or settings (exit 139, RCCL timeout, node fault), inside the arm-seed cap.
  - No failed arm is rerun to fill GPUs.
- Every seed of every arm runs regardless of earlier development results, subject only to the budget below. The soups
  need both seeds, and the development panels cannot decide the questions.
