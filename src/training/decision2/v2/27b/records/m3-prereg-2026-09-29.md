# ~27B track, Milestone 3: preregistration (2026-09-29)

Signed before any Milestone 3 GPU run. Scope is the coordinator's approved
Milestone 3 (04:05 note; about 32 GPU-hours on node B GPU5–6, GPU7 after the
research & data H7 / H8 wave, more GPUs from the 9B track when released).
Development readouts (SELECT, CAL, typed DEV, CSS pilot, arm held-out slices)
are never release scores; JevArena v3 is post-key and reported as "post-key
same-panel"; public231 is a public-subset rerun, not the official JevBench
rank. Nothing is published. Release stays HOLD unless a formal result meets the
~27B first-release bar: **post-key v3 ≥ 64.9** (90% of AutoJev-27B's 72.133 on
the node B kernel image), human transfer not significantly below AutoJev-27B,
and no decision type collapsed.

## Question

Milestone 2's best candidate (Score arm A0s ∪ A6g/A6h, seed 1; v3 62.72) trails
the bar on Choice-side typed reasoning over families it never saw: constraint
competition .388 and exception stack .623 vs AutoJev-27B's 1.000 / .843.
**Does adding A7's Stage 1–4 typed curricula (A7g / A7o / A7p / A7i of
`a7-dec10-v3`, never seen by a Qwen-start 27B) to the strict-base Score arm
raise typed reasoning on the unseen formal families, against the same Score arm
repeated to the same number of tokens?**

## Arms (two seeds each: s1 = 20260926, s2 = 20260928)

- **M3-S (control: the Milestone 2 Score arm on the strict base, matched
  tokens).** Base A0s-strict (all rows admitted at 4,096) ∪ A6 = A6g ∪ A6h as
  one pool at ρ = 50% of the base's tokens (whole groups, seeded stratified
  prefix, Milestone 2's `A6:rho`). The whole mixture is then repeated (seeded
  reshuffle per pass, `#rK` id suffixes, the last pass a seeded whole-group
  prefix) until its token total matches M3-A's within ±0.5%.
- **M3-A (treatment).** The identical base ∪ A6 rows once, plus an A7 slice of
  **D = 5.0M native tokens**. Rows come from A7g ∪ A7o ∪ A7p ∪ A7i TRAIN, at
  most 4,096 tokens each; an A7 group is dropped whole if its `group_id` or any
  row's `input_sha256` is already in the mixture (2,545 A7 inputs duplicate
  A0s-strict rows: A7g 1,987, A7p 349, A7i 209). Every `family` gets an equal
  integer token quota (water-filling: a family smaller than the quota
  contributes all its rows and the remainder is re-split among the others),
  with whole groups in seeded order within each family; a quota is filled by
  Milestone 2's rule (the longest group prefix at or below the quota, plus the
  next group if that lands closer).
  - Equal per-family quotas maximize the number of distinct generated families
    at a fixed dose (the hypothesis is transfer to unseen families) and keep the
    long `relations` / `dense_table` families from dominating.
  - A7i enters as its own families under the same rule, as the coordinator
    approved the four sub-arms together.
- Expected size: ≈ 5.04M (base ∪ A6) + 5.0M (A7) ≈ 10.0M tokens per run, about
  1,350 updates. The dose follows the approved proposal (about 5M A7 tokens);
  the full A7g/o/p/i pool at 4,096 is ≈ 102.7M tokens (≈ 43 GPU-hours per run).
- **XL r2 as the data base: considered, not run.** mx-xl-full-r2 is 179.2M
  tokens (≈ 75 GPU-hours per seed at ≈ 2.4M tokens per GPU-hour). A
  token-matched subsample would replace the A0s-strict base with mostly data-v2
  and multilingual rows aimed at human transfer, where the 27B is already level
  with the peer, and would carry only ≈ 23% Stage 1–4 tokens. The A7 arm tests
  the gap directly.

## Preflights and stop rules (per arm-seed, as in Milestone 2)

- Mixtures are built twice into separate directories on node B and must be
  byte-identical; the mixture manifest (hashes; rows and tokens per source,
  family, language and type) is frozen by amendment 1 before any launch.
- Admission (every row of the mixture file admitted at 4,096; the builder
  already excludes the 131 base rows over the limit), one-step, reload (32 SELECT rows,
  0 argmax changes, max |Δp| ≤ 1e-4). A failed preflight stops that arm-seed;
  it is recorded and not rerun.
- Caps: 5.0 GPU-hours per full attempt and 6.0 per arm-seed (Milestone 2: 3.0 /
  3.2 for 5M tokens). A nonfinite loss, an OOM or a cap breach stops the
  arm-seed.
- Both seeds of both arms run regardless of seed 1's development results: the
  soups need both seeds, and the development panels cannot test the hypothesis
  (next section). Only the failures above stop an arm-seed.

## Development readout (every arm-seed BEST and every soup)

- Through the eval runner with the 27B kernel adapter at the Milestone 3 limit:
  typed DEV and CSS pilot, then `v2.eval.dev_readout` (P = 100·√(T_dev ·
  H_pilot)); CAL698 temperature fit on the kernel path at the limit
  (`v2/27b/kernel_readout.py`; CAL698 metrics in `cal698.summary.json`);
  SELECT700 probabilities from the trainer at BEST for arm-seeds and from the
  kernel path at the limit for soups; AHO-A6g / A6h / A7 at BEST (`aho_eval`,
  arm-seeds only, since it needs a training run directory). AHO-A7 may hold
  fewer than 800 rows (two-row A7i groups leave small gaps under a family's
  quota).
- **What the development panels cannot show:** typed-DEV Choice is at ceiling
  for every 27B LoRA arm so far (.989–1.000), and the formal families where the
  gap sits (constraint competition, exception stack) have no development
  counterpart. So the development contrast checks retention and shows
  within-generator learning (AHO-A7); the hypothesis is decided on the formal
  panel under the finalist cap.
- **Contrast (paired bootstrap, 10,000 draws; per seed and pooled):** Δ T_dev,
  per-type Δ, Δ H_pilot, Δ P_dev, Δ CAL698 Brier, Δ AHO. Retention floors vs
  M3-S, pooled over seeds: no typed-DEV type down more than 3.0 points, H_pilot
  down at most 1.5 points, CAL698 Brier worse by at most 0.010, invalid answers
  not increased. A floor breach is reported and disclosed; it does not by itself
  keep a candidate from the formal runner (only the proxy rule below does).

## Soups and candidate artifacts

- For each arm, the exact uniform soup of its two seeds' BEST checkpoints. LoRA
  factors are concatenated along the rank (A′ = [A₁; A₂], B′ = [B₁, B₂] / 2,
  r′ = 16, α′ = 32), so the soup's update equals the mean of the seed updates
  exactly. Non-LoRA trainable tensors (the decision head) are averaged. The
  result is a `qwen-adapter` checkpoint on the pinned base.
- Verified before use: on every projection, max |ΔW_soup − mean ΔW_seeds| ≤
  1e-6 × max |mean ΔW_seeds| (FP32), and the release builder's adapter checks
  pass.
- Candidate artifact per arm (17:15 seed rule): the soup if P_dev(soup) ≥ the
  mean of its seeds' P_dev; otherwise the seed with the higher SELECT700 score.

## Finalists and formal runs (post-key same-panel)

- **Proxy screen (proxy v2):** a candidate artifact is dropped only if its P_dev
  is ≥ 8 below the best Milestone 3 candidate's. The proxy over-predicts 27B
  LoRA arms by about 4–5 v3 points and is never used to pick among siblings.
- **At most three formal finalists; two planned.** F1 = M3-A's candidate
  artifact; F2 = M3-S's candidate artifact (the matched control and itself a
  strict-base candidate). A third needs an amendment before its collection.
- **Package:** CAL698 temperatures fitted at the Milestone 3 limit with
  `v2/release/calibrate_frozen.py` (extended for adapter checkpoints, kernels
  required). They are ADOPTED only if they do not worsen aggregate ECE and
  Brier on typed DEV and the CSS pilot (`v2/release/dev_calibration.py`, 23:15
  rule; decided on development panels before the formal collection); otherwise
  T = 1. The package (checkpoint, adopted calibration, limit) is frozen before
  its formal collection.
- **Formal run:** eval runner, kernel adapter, a fresh copy of the frozen cache,
  an 8-item smoke per panel, then collection (typed FINAL, CSS15, public231),
  seal, report.
- **Paired comparisons (5,000 draws):**
  - against AutoJev-27B (node B kernel run, v3 72.133), Eikos-27B (69.290) and
    Jebadiah (65.472);
  - F1 vs F2: the post-key effect of A7 at matched tokens;
  - against Milestone 2's M2-S s1, descriptive only because the runtime
    differs.
- **Release gate (~27B):** v3 ≥ 64.9; human transfer not significantly below
  AutoJev-27B (the paired 95% CI of ΔH includes 0 or lies above it); no
  decision type collapsed (every type above chance on typed FINAL, and more
  than one answer category used). If F1 or F2 passes, stage its artifact
  privately (`qwen-adapter`, pinned base; storage policy: soups and finalists
  only, headroom checked first) and report immediately with the scored run
  directory.
- **Attribution:** F1 − F2 on post-key v3 and T, with paired 95% CIs and the
  four FINAL families. "A7 lifts unseen-family typed reasoning" only if the
  lower bound of ΔT is above 0.
