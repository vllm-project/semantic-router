# ~27B track, Milestone 4: proposal (2026-09-29; not approved, not run)

**Target: the typed-reasoning gap to AutoJev-27B.**

- **The gap is all in T.** F1 (M3-A soup, post-key v3 67.21) trails AutoJev-27B
  by −4.92 v3 (−6.82, −0.43). That splits into typed T −.099 and human
  transfer H −.013 (not significant). At F1's H, AutoJev's T would give
  v3 ≈ 71.3.
- **Within T, it is two families.** Constraint competition is .570 vs 1.000
  and exception stack .788 vs .843. Evidence join is level (1.000), resource
  ledger favours F1 (.793 vs .705), and Score favours F1 (317 vs 282 of 400).
- **M3's matched control points at dose.** About 5M A7 Stage 1–4 tokens
  bought +.188 on constraint competition and +.200 on exception stack
  (F1 − F2; ΔT +.099 (+.081, +.118)). They also moved H −.030 (−.080,
  +.023). So the dose of A7 is the main lever, adapter capacity is the second,
  and H needs a guard.
- **What has already failed.** AutoJev-27B KL on A0s prompts (M2-K) did not
  reach either family (.383 / .557).

**Arms.** Two seeds each, on the M3 template:

- Template: rank-8 LoRA on the official Qwen3.8-27B, strict base, the same A6
  rows, 4,096-token training rows, exact seed soups, and T = 1 unless CAL698
  passes the 23:15 rule. Readouts on the kernel path at 32K. Formal runs on
  node B with image `dbe5f32b` and a copy of F1's scored cache.
- **M4-A20:** A0s-strict ∪ A6 ∪ 20M A7 tokens, four times M3's dose, with the
  same equal-per-family water-filling and deduplication against the base.
  About 25M tokens in total.
- **M4-A20r:** the same mixture at LoRA rank 32 (α 64), to test adapter
  capacity.
- **Control M4-Ar:** M3-A's exact mixture repeated to the same ≈ 25M tokens.
  It separates new A7 content from simply more updates.
- **Incumbent:** F1 is the paired comparator.

**Decision rule, declared up front.**

- A successor replaces F1 only if all of these hold:
  - its paired v3 CI lower bound vs F1 is > 0;
  - its H vs F1 is not significantly lower;
  - every no-1.0 gate holds.
- Attribution, on T and the four FINAL families:
  - content = M4-A20 − M4-Ar;
  - capacity = M4-A20r − M4-A20.
