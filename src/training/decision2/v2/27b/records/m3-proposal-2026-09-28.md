# ~27B track, Milestone 3: proposal (draft, not launched)

Written during Milestone 2 from its evidence (`m2-results-2026-09-28.md`); it
runs only after coordinator approval and a new preregistration.

## What Milestone 2 established at 27B

- **Packaging is not the gap.** The incumbent at 8,192 tokens scores exactly
  what it scored at 4,096 (post-key v3 56.747); the recovered inputs sit in
  low-scoring tasks away from the median.
- **Score data is the largest measured lever.** A6g ∪ A6h at matched tokens
  lifts typed-DEV Score by 23.5 points and T_dev by 6.4 (development
  readouts) — the typed-family gap on unseen generators is where AutoJev-27B
  leads by .28 T.
- **The strict-isolation base plus longer training is a real post-key gain**
  (A0s control 60.09 vs the incumbent's 56.75, +3.3 [+1.3, +6.4]); human
  transfer is level with AutoJev-27B.
- **Generated verifiable rules (A2) do not reach unseen typed families** at
  this dose, although they lift their own held-out families.
- Measured cost: rank-8 LoRA on one MI325X trains about 2.47M native tokens
  per GPU-hour (FLA, shared autotune cache, batch 16, 4,096-token cap).

## Proposed arms (two seeds each, template S discipline, A0s-strict pk1 base)

1. **Score at scale:** A0s-strict ∪ the mixture-v2 Score slices (H6 human,
   G6 generated, v1 A6g/A6h) ∪ A7's human Score sub-arms (A7q, A7k, A7s) at
   about 8M tokens, against A0s-strict repeated to the same tokens.
2. **Typed curricula that are new to this lineage:** the A7 Stage 1–4
   generated families (A7g / A7o / A7p / A7i; never seen by a Qwen-start 27B,
   and the source of the 0.8B candidate's typed gains) at about 8M tokens,
   same control.
3. **Teacher:** the better of AutoJev-27B and own-Lux KL from Milestone 2
   (only if one passes), applied to the winning mixture, with the matched
   own-Lux control that AutoJev-distilled candidates need.
4. **Combined candidate:** the passing arms together at mixture-v2 full-M
   size (19.7M tokens), packaged at the largest limit the runtime supports
   (32,768 admits every CSS15 input) with same-limit controls, CAL698
   calibration, one formal run on node B.

## Compute request

Each 8M-token arm is about 3.6 GPU-hours with preflights and readout; two
seeds for two arms plus one shared two-seed control is about 22 GPU-hours, and
the full-M candidate about 9. On node B GPU5–6 alone that is about 16 wall
hours; four more GPUs for one day would halve it.
