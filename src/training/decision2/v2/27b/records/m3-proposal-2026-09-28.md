# ~27B track, Milestone 3: proposal (not launched)

Written at the end of Milestone 2 from its evidence (`m2-results-2026-09-28.md`);
it runs only after coordinator approval and a new preregistration.

## What Milestone 2 established at 27B (node B, post-key same-panel unless noted)

- **Packaging is not the gap.** The incumbent at 8,192 tokens scores exactly
  what it scored at 4,096 (v3 56.747).
- **Score data is the largest measured lever.** A6g ∪ A6h at matched tokens:
  typed-DEV Score +23.5 / +10.8 points on two seeds; the formal candidate
  (seed 1) reaches **v3 62.72** vs its matched control 60.09 (+2.63 [+0.65,
  +6.85]) and C0 56.75; the Score family now beats AutoJev-27B (resource
  ledger .728 vs .705). Bar: 64.9 (AutoJev-27B 72.133 on the kernel image).
- **The remaining gap is Choice-side typed reasoning on unseen families:**
  constraint competition .388 vs 1.000 and exception stack .623 vs .843.
  Human transfer is level with the peer (leads on 10 of 15 tasks).
- **Generated verifiable rules (A2) do not transfer** at this dose.
- **AutoJev-27B KL on A0s prompts is not the missing piece.** It ties the
  Score arm on the development proxy (74.18 vs 74.73) but post-key it is level
  with the hard-label control (v3 60.31 vs 60.09) and leaves constraint
  competition at .383; the proxy over-predicts both treatments.
- Cost: rank-8 LoRA on one MI325X trains about 2.47M native tokens per
  GPU-hour; each 5M-token arm with preflights and readout is about 2.6 GPU-h,
  each formal run about 0.6.

## Proposed arms (A0s-strict pk1 base, two seeds, template-S discipline)

1. **Typed curricula new to this lineage (primary):** A7's Stage 1–4
   generated families (A7g / A7o / A7p / A7i; never seen by a Qwen-start 27B;
   the source of the 0.8B candidate's typed Choice gains) at about 5M tokens
   on top of A0s-strict ∪ A6g/A6h(ρ), against that Score-arm mixture repeated
   to the same tokens — the most direct test for constraint competition and
   exception stacks, where the whole remaining gap sits.
2. **Score arm on the strict base:** A0s-strict ∪ A6g/A6h(ρ), two seeds, so
   the release candidate carries neither the two shortcut families nor the
   A0s caveat; a uniform soup of its seeds if it beats their mean.
   Teacher KL only as the preregistered own-Lux trust region if M2-L beats
   hard labels; AutoJev targets are not prioritized given M2-K's formal
   readout.
3. **Scale:** the passing arms together at mixture-v2 full-S size (8M tokens)
   or larger, packaged at 32,768 tokens (admits every CSS15 input) with
   same-limit controls, CAL698 calibration, one formal run on node B; seed
   soup of the candidate's two seeds if it beats the seed mean on development
   panels.

## Compute request

About 24 GPU-hours for arms 1–2 (two seeds plus shared controls) and 8 for the
combined candidate and its formal run: roughly 16 wall hours on node B
GPU5–6, or about 6 with four more GPUs.
