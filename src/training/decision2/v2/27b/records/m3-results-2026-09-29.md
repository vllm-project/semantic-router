## Incident: the F2 chain never ran (found 14:17, recorded 15:10 UTC+8)

- **What was promised (11:50):** this record, gist 06 (entry 12:10) and the
  worker's handoff all said that after M3-S s2 "the M3-S soup and F2 … and the
  development contrast" run automatically, ETA ≈ 14:40 UTC+8. Results were
  expected in `M3-S-soup/formal` and `m3-contrast/contrast.json`.
- **What node B showed at 14:17:**
  - Both chain scripts were 0 bytes: `m3-logs/m3-s-side.sh` (written 03:05:28Z)
    and `m3-logs/m3-final-score.sh` (03:48:49Z).
  - Their logs `s-side.log` and `final-score.log` were created 2 ms after the
    scripts and are empty.
  - M3-S s2 trained normally (`COMPLETE.json` 05:25:49Z, full attempt 3.998
    GPU-hours).
  - Nothing else ran: no M3-S-s2 readout, soup, `m3-contrast/` or F2
    directory, no chain process, and every GPU on both nodes was idle.
  - The sibling scripts `m3-build.sh`, `launch-arm.sh` and `m3-queue.sh`
    (uploaded one per ssh call) have content.
- **Cause (high confidence).** Each script was uploaded and launched in one ssh
  command of the form `cat > script && chmod +x script && … setsid nohup
  ./script … &`.
  - The trailing `&` backgrounds the whole `&&` list, including `cat`.
  - The remote non-interactive shell gives background jobs `/dev/null` as
    input, so `cat` wrote 0 bytes. The empty script then ran and exited at
    once.
  - Reproduced read-only over the same ssh path: `'wc -c' < file` reads the
    file's bytes, `'wc -c & sleep 2' < file` reads 0.
  - The worker's local copies were intact (2,257 and 786 bytes).
  - The "still alive" checks used `pgrep -f <script name>`, which matches the
    remote ssh shell's own command line, so they always passed. Nothing checked
    file sizes or first log lines.
- **Classification:** a broken chain, not a preflight failure. None of the
  F2 steps had started, so completing them is not a rerun.
- **Remedy:**
  - The coordinator's 14:25 chain rule: before handing off an automatic chain,
    check that each script is non-empty and matches its local SHA-256, that
    each job has started (process or container), and that its first log line
    exists; record the check in the handoff.
  - Upload and launch are separate ssh calls; the upload is checked by size and
    SHA-256.
  - F2 is completed under amendment 4 (drivers in `v2/27b/m3f2/`).
  - Cost of the gap: about 1 h of idle node-B 27B GPUs (13:25–14:28 UTC+8) and
    a late F2.
