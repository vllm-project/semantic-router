## Incidents and fixes (no result affected)

- **Shared contrast output (found by reading the code, 15:55Z; no chain had reached it).** The four chains share
  `m6/gates/`, and each chain's `m6-gates.sh gates` ends with `m4_contrast --output gates/contrast.json`, which
  refuses an existing file. Every chain after the first one with a sealed finalist would have stopped there, before
  its overlap and verdict steps. The running chains cannot take new code, so a guard on node B (`m6-contrast-guard.sh`,
  `b980dd144`) moves each complete `contrast.json` aside within 5 s; nothing reads that file downstream. The
  attribution over all finalists gets its own `m6-gates.sh contrast` stage.
