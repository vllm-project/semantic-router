# A7 (own Decision 1.0 training corpora): preregistration v1 (2026-09-28)

Signed before any A7 row is built or selected. It fixes the sources, admission
rules, sub-arms, audits and outputs of data arm A7, version `a7-dec10-v1`.
Frozen results (hashes, counts, receipts, HF revision) go to
`a7-arms-v1-2026-09-28.md`; they may not change these rules. A rule change is a
new version with its own preregistration. The corpus inventory that motivates
this arm is `a7-inventory-2026-09-28.md`.

Scope of v1: the **decoder-family corpora** that trained Sol, Nox, Eos and Lux
and supplied Kai's final shared Choice/Score TRAIN (16,529 of the 24,000-row
natural-reading pool). The encoder-family corpora (Kai/Lex history, 79 rosters)
are inventoried only; their arms need a separate version because their label
provenance is heterogeneous (some teacher-derived or agent-authored parts).

A7 reuses the research & data track's tools unchanged (`v2.data.overlap`,
`v2.data.shortcut`, `v2.data.freeze`) and never edits arms A0–A6.

## 2. Admission rules

1. **Training rights.** A source is admitted only if its terms permit model
   training: project-generated text (1.0 generators, no third-party text);
   Cosmos QA and BANKING77 (CC BY 4.0); CLINC150 (CC BY 3.0); SNLI and SQuAD 2.0
   (CC BY-SA 4.0); MultiNLI government/slate/telephone/travel genres (OANC
   permissive terms per the dataset card pinned at
   `nyu-mll/multi_nli@da70db2af9d09693783c3320c4249840212ee221`; fiction is not
   used). Attribution and share-alike obligations are carried per source in the
   A7 licence registry.
2. **Redistribution.** Sources whose terms permit redistribution go to the
   private HF dataset; any admitted source without redistribution rights stays
   under `/data/dev2/private/a7/` on the nodes with its hash recorded. (All v1
   sources above permit redistribution with attribution/share-alike.)
3. **Label provenance.** Admitted: publisher-shipped human annotations,
   program-oracle labels of project generators, and own Decision 1.0 teacher
   outputs. Not admitted: labels (or model-written states) produced by a closed
   third-party API whose terms forbid training competing models, or by an
   unidentified model whose terms cannot be verified. Excluded volumes are
   reported separately so the coordinator can weigh them.
4. **Protected overlap.** `v2.data.overlap` against PI-v2
   (`/data/dev2/private/data/pi-v2/manifest.json`, SHA-256
   `835b0df9bcddef084d53e20aac2ceb8576a0eb5cb94de79a27907e24e1484572`, 39 roles,
   including the >800-character long-leaf windows). Any non-boilerplate hit
   against any role other than `rights_clean_train` quarantines the whole
   candidate group; `rights_clean_train` hits are reported as cross-arm
   duplication. The GPU embedding scan of data-arms v1 is requested from the
   coordinator (A7 is CPU-only); until it runs it is recorded as pending, not
   passed.
5. **Item-group isolation.** Groups connected by a shared `input_sha256` or a
   shared normalized state (at least 20 compact characters) form one component.
   Components are assigned whole to TRAIN or AHO. A component that touches A0
   TRAIN (group id or input hash) is never AHO. `v2.data.freeze isolation`
   across A7 TRAIN/AHO, SELECT700, CAL700, A0 TRAIN and every published v2 arm
   and replay file must PASS.
6. **Deduplication.** One row per `input_sha256`. When an input occurs in more
   than one source, the first sub-arm in the order A7h, A7m, A7g, A7i, A7p, A7o
   keeps it. Identical inputs with different labels are all dropped and counted.
7. **Construction-defect exclusions (generated rows only).**
   a. The 1.0 team's documented legacy numeric-choice rule: Choice rows of the
      eight legacy numeric families `arithmetic`, `stage3_arithmetic_difference`,
      `stage3_arithmetic_distance`, `stage3_arithmetic_net`,
      `stage3_arithmetic_product`, `stage3_arithmetic_sum`,
      `stage3_transition_registers`, `stage3_transition_scalar` are excluded
      (midrange/absolute-value priors; their Noul rows remain eligible).
   b. Stage1/Stage2 Noul and Score rows with opaque option keys (not `false/true`
      or `0..K-1`) are held: no semantics-preserving key mapping is verified.
   c. Shortcut gate at family × task type: a generated cell with at least 30 rows
      whose `state_removed` or `option_only` accuracy exceeds its cross-validated
      majority by more than 5 points (`v2.data.shortcut` receipt, the data-arms v1
      margin) is dropped from its sub-arm. For programmatic data the state is
      the only legitimate evidence, so such a cell leaks its answer.
8. **Native budget.** A group containing a row above 8,192 native tokens
   (`Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base@dc7cdfe2`, repository `encode`) is excluded. Rows
   above 1,024 Kai tokens are flagged T1a-ineligible, not removed.
9. **Diagnostics (disclosed, not blocking).** Shortcut views of human sub-arms
   (including hypothesis-only), TRAIN↔AHO near-duplicate self-scan, language and
   token mix, Score level counts, Choice gold positions, and a mechanism-
   similarity note versus the typed DEV/FINAL families. A diagnostic becomes
   blocking only if it shows leakage or broken labels, recorded as a new version.
10. **Freeze.** `v2.data.freeze` per file (rows sorted by id, canonical JSON per
    line); tokenizers as data-arms v1 (`qwen3-0.6b-base@da87bfb6`,
    `qwen3.5-0.8b-base@dc7cdfe2`, raw `kai-0.6b@7185f514`); licence registry
    `v2/data/a7/license-registry-a7-v1.json`. Built only on a node from an exact
    mirror of a pushed commit.
11. **Held-out slice.** AHO = components with
    `int(sha256(component_key), 16) % 10 == 0`, where `component_key` is the
    smallest group id in the component (split `select`, diagnostic only).
    Checkpoint selection stays on SELECT700. A track that replicates an exact
    1.0 mixture may train on TRAIN ∪ AHO of a view and must then not report AHO.
