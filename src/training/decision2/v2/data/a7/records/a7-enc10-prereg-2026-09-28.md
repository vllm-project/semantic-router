# A7 encoder-family corpora: preregistration `a7-enc10-v1` (2026-09-28)

Signed before any `a7-enc10-v1` row is built. It covers the Kai/Lex (encoder)
side of the Decision 1.0 corpora that `a7-prereg-2026-09-28.md` deferred, under
the coordinator decision of 2026-09-28 16:00: **admit human-labelled data only;
do not admit rubric, question or schema text written by an unrecorded AI agent;
rebuild with deterministic templates around the human labels.** Frozen results
go to `a7-enc10-arms-2026-09-28.md`; they may not change these rules.

## 1. What is (not) reused from the 1.0 rosters

The 1.0 rosters (inventory: 79 rosters, 1.78M rows incl. oversampling) serve
only to choose sources. **No 1.0 question text, level/option description, schema
translation or state framing is reused**: several are agent-authored (OASST
es/zh rubrics, the Hindi SentiMix schema, MASSIVE/STS native question frames).
States are re-rendered from the pinned upstream files; instructions and option
descriptions are fixed English templates in `v2/data/a7/build_enc.py` (authored
in this repository, one template per family, the scaffolding rule of data-arms
v2 §1.5); labels are the publishers' human labels.

## 3. Level rules (Score)

- Ties: a row whose scaled mean lies within 0.1 of a level boundary (k + 0.5) is
  dropped (A7q on 4v, A7k on s).
- Balance: within each family, every level keeps at most 1.2 × the rarest
  level's rows (the data-arms v1 human-ordinal rule), rows chosen in
  `sha256("<family>:" + id)` order. A7x balances gold intents per (locale,
  scenario) the same way, then keeps at most 4,000 rows per locale in
  `sha256(utterance id)` order (the same utterances across locales), and rotates
  the gold position by `sha256(id) % 4`.
- Pairs of A7k that share a normalized sentence with an excluded research & data
  pair are excluded too.

## 4. Gates (as prereg §2 with PI-v3)

Deduplication by input hash (identical inputs with different labels dropped);
isolation (no group, input hash or normalized state shared with SELECT700,
CAL700, a v1 arm AHO slice, A0 or any published TRAIN file; components touching
a TRAIN file are never AHO); four-method lexical overlap against PI-v3 without
TRAIN roles with whole-group quarantine; native budget 8,192 tokens (Kai >1,024
flagged); shortcut: **every family × type cell with ≥ 30 rows whose state-removed
or option-only accuracy exceeds its cross-validated majority by more than 5
points is dropped** (the stricter default-mixture rule; an ablation copy may be
published separately and labelled so); the GPU embedding scan (amendment 2
settings) before any sub-arm is marked release-ready, until then
development-only. AHO = components with `sha256(min group id) % 10 == 0`.
Freeze, isolation against every published partition (including `a7-dec10-v3`
and A7r), licence registry `license-registry-a7-v2.json`, upload under
`v2/a7/arms/<sub>/` of the private dataset.
