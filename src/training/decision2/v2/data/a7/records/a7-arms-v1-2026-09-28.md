## Audits (diagnostics unless stated)

- **Shortcut (post-admission, `audits/<sub>/post-shortcut.json`):** A7g, A7m, A7i,
  A7p, A7o PASS every gated view (e.g. A7g Choice option-only 0.182 vs majority
  0.180; A7i option-only 0.131 vs 0.122, was 0.473 in v1). **A7h FAILS** on known
  source artifacts, disclosed and kept (prereg §2.9): Cosmos QA option-only +11.9
  and state-removed +10.8 points; SQuAD 2.0 answerability question-only +6.4.
  Consumers wanting data-arms-v1 parity drop families `natural_cosmos_qa` and
  `natural_squad2_answerability`. The hashed-feature learner puts SNLI and
  MultiNLI hypothesis-only at chance; this does not establish absence of the
  literature's hypothesis-only bias.
- **Protected overlap:** PI-v2 (39 roles, 40,857 rows; long leaves windowed,
  `protected_leaves_over_800_chars_not_near_scanned = 0`). Generated sub-arms hit no
  protected role. The GPU embedding scan of data-arms v1 has **not** been run for
  A7 (CPU-only track); requested from the coordinator.
- **TRAIN↔AHO self-scan:** generated sub-arms share templates (A7g 23,266 rare
  n-gram pairs, 0 exact; A7o 14,225 n-gram, 16 exact); AHO is a within-generator
  diagnostic, not an independent test. Human sub-arms: A7h 13 exact, 180 short-near;
  A7i 198 short-near (similar utterances); A7m 105 n-gram pairs.
- **Language and token mix:** A7g/A7p/A7o are half English, half Chinese; A7h/A7m/A7i
  English only. A7g carries 74% of A7 tokens (≈1,645 tokens/row versus ≈228 for
  A7h); mix by sub-arm under a token budget. 36,423 A7g, 420 A7o and 140 A7p rows
  exceed 1,024 Kai tokens (T1a-ineligible, not removed).
- **Mechanism similarity to typed DEV/FINAL (disclosure):** Stage4 automaton and
  registers resemble `transition_table` / `resource_ledger`; scope resembles
  `rule_precedence` / `exception_stack` / `evidence_join`; Boolean resembles
  `attribute_gate`. The generators, templates and entities differ (text overlap is
  zero) and `constraint_competition` has no 1.0 counterpart. Gains on v3 typed after
  A7g are partly same-mechanism practice, as they were for the 1.0 comparators.

## Recommended first use

The 9B study found that Lux's own head beats a head retrained on A0 by 21.9 points
on identical frozen features. `dec10-natural24k` is the exact Lux/Eos pool
(23,222 of 24,000 rows after admission); a head retrained on it (same features,
same budget) directly tests whether TRAIN breadth explains that gap. Larger
tiers can then add A7g (Score L3–L8) and A7m/A7i as separately ablatable arms.
