## 3. Validity check (prereg §7): **NOT met**

DEV2.0-4B and Decider 4B were collected on `hs1-dev` with the frozen runner on node A GPU1. The settings:

- **DEV2.0-4B:** release package `452f1332…`, T = 1, 16K, fresh N4XF autotune cache.
- **Decider 4B:** `eb5fbdfc…`, published settings.
- **Result:** 0 invalid answers for either model. The collection took 0.107 GPU-h.

| Family | Decider 4B | DEV2.0-4B | Δ | Paired cluster 95% CI | Valid |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| F1 quote check (958) | .549 | .534 | +.015 | [−.020, +.049] | no |
| F2 policy packet (478) | .630 | .609 | +.021 | [−.023, +.065] | no |
| F3 unmet condition (960) | .794 | .774 | +.020 | [−.006, +.046] | not gated |

- **Point estimates:** Decider leads on all three families and on 8 of 10 family × interface cells (DEV2.0-4B is ahead on F1 and F2 Score), but no interval
  excludes 0. Under the preregistered rule, **F1 and F2 are published flagged "dev slice not validated"**.
- **Behavioural diagnostics mirror the public-231 finding:**
  - **F1 adopt rate:** DEV2.0-4B agrees with the quoted answer on .775 of rows, against Decider's .641. A perfect
    model scores .50.
  - **F3 false-yes on unmet conditions:** DEV2.0-4B .262, Decider .128, which meets the preregistered expectation.
- **Both models are weak on F1** (.53–.55; Noul .55–.59 against a .50 baseline; dev-ood .46–.47). F2 is easier for
  both (~.61–.63) than the public long-policy items (3 and 10 of 19). A floor effect in F1 and the modest size of the
  slices limit the accuracy contrast.
- **The single allowed amendment was not used.** The observed Δ is about .015–.02. A lower bound above 0 would need
  roughly 4–5× larger F1/F2 slices, about 0.25 GPU-h, which is more than the 0.19 GPU-h left. Redesigning the
  generators after seeing model outputs is what the rule forbids.

## 5. How tracks should use HS1

- **Where it goes.** Add `m4/hs1/train.jsonl` at `171e6f0c…` as its own block. A running milestone needs a committed
  amendment; otherwise add it at the next milestone.
- **Suggested dose.** All 19,968 rows (~14M native tokens; about 7–8% of an XL-r2-sized mixture), with a
  matched-token control. For a lighter dose, take F2 + F3 plus half of F1 (about 9M tokens).
- **Quarantine carried forward.** Keep the 4B HotpotQA near-match group quarantined, as the eval record requires.
- **What to read.**
  - `hs1-dev` in development (`--panels hs1-dev`; score with `python3 -m v2.data.hs1.validity`).
  - Report accuracy per family and per dev-id / dev-ood, plus the F1 adopt rate and the F3 false-yes rate.
  - Since the accuracy contrast is not validated, treat the **adopt rate and false-yes rate** as the primary skill
    readouts. A successful HS1 arm should move them toward .50 and toward 0.
  - Public-231 hard (long policy and planted-conclusion subsets) stays the formal out-of-sample check under
    successor rule item 7. Nobody trains on `dev.jsonl` or selects on public 231.
- **Labels only.** Rows carry gold labels only; teacher targets would need their own preregistration.
