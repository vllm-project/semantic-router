## Fresh blind review

**Sample.** 225 items from the pass-3 TRAIN file: 19 per family, except `sumedit` with 16 (its 576 rows sit in 29
groups). The round-1 screened and reviewed rows and their groups were excluded, and the salts and item prefix were
new.

**Reviewers.** Five fresh subagents (`claude-opus-5-5-max`), all run in the foreground (`run_in_background: false`)
with the round-1 instructions:

- R1 and R2 each answered both packets, in different item orders;
- R3 answered the 4 splits.

There was no deviation from the protocol apart from the addendum above.

| Measure | Result | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| Errors | 12 / 225 = 5.33% (exact 95% CI 2.79–9.13%; Wilson 3.08–9.09%) | ≤ 5% and upper ≤ 8%: **P1 fail** |
| Population-weighted | 5.97% (bootstrap 95% CI 2.61–9.77%) | ≤ 5%: **P2 fail** |
| Per family | `wands` 4 / 19 (exact lower bound above 5%) | no family over: **P3 fail** |

**Errors by family:**

- `wands` 4: all answered `Partial`;
- `sumedit` 3: two "consistent" golds where the reviewers saw the summary as loose but acceptable, and one hedged
  claim stated as fact;
- `csqa` 2;
- `sentfin` 1: answered neutral;
- `snips_sel` 1;
- `w2c` 1.

Agreement with gold was 94.2% (R1) and 93.3% (R2). Between reviewers, raw agreement was 98.2% and κ was .980.

**Reading.** In 5 of the 12 errors, both reviewers chose the class that round 2 removed from gold but kept as an option
(`wands` `Partial` 4, `sentfin` neutral 1). Amendment 2 kept the option sets to change nothing but the rows, and that
choice accounts for P3 and most of the gap to P1. Without those 5 items the error rate would be 7 / 225. That figure
is not a result of this round, which is decided as preregistered.
