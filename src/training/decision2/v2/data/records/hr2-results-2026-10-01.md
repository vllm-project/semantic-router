## Blind label review (§4 as amended)

216 TRAIN rows (24 per family, one per group, gold-stratified), two packets of 108 per reviewer order; R1 and R2
fresh `claude-opus-5-5-max` subagents with `records/dq/reviewer-hr2.md`, R3 fresh on the 3 split items.

- Errors 13 / 216 = 6.02% [3.24, 10.07] (P1 needs ≤ 9); weighted 6.50% [3.09, 10.40] (P2 ≤ 5%); per family:
  VitaminC 4, `hs3_help` 3, `eth_just` 2, `allegro` 1, `eth_cs` 1, `hs3_pref` 1, `prm_step` 1, `eth_deon` 0,
  `eth_util` 0 (P3 fails at ≥ 5). By type: Choice 1 / 48, Noul 8 / 120, Score 4 / 48.
- Agreement with gold: R1 93.5%, R2 94.0%, final 94.0%; R1–R2 raw 97.2%, κ .966 (one model family: the reviewers
  agree with each other far more than with gold). No defect flags.
- Error pattern: the four VitaminC errors are all gold "refutes" rows both reviewers read as supported (three with
  high confidence from both); the three `hs3_help` errors are two levels from the human median. Noul direction:
  false "no" 5, false "yes" 3. Limitation: AI adjudication, not human annotation.
- Receipts: `records/hr2/audits/review.public.json`, `review-sample.json`; the error ids stay on node A.

## How tracks should add HR2

Add `m5/hr2/hr2.train.jsonl@afc3bc1e` as **one block**, by amendment or at the next milestone, against a
**matched-token control** (the same base mixture plus the same ~16.8M native tokens drawn from its own rows),
screened with **HT-DEV v2** (no FLAG vs the tier reference). Read `hr2.dev.jsonl` per family as a diagnostic only
(never selection); formal CSS15 decides human transfer, and items 1–8 apply; item 8 needs the custodian C1 recheck
first. **Because HR2 is not release-safe, a successor trained on it cannot be released** until a separately
preregistered and reviewed round replaces it. Candidates for such a round, for the coordinator: drop or re-filter
VitaminC "refutes" rows and `hs3_help` (4 and 3 of the 13 errors), then re-review with the same thresholds.
