## Blind review (prereg §4)

- **Stage S (screen):** 10 TRAIN rows per family (120), one fresh `claude-opus-5-5-max` reviewer with
  `dq/reviewer-ib1.md`: 4 disagreements (`sentfin`, `snips_rel`, `sumedit`, `w2c` one each); no family reached 3;
  the 4 rows leave TRAIN.
- **Stage R (decisive):** a fresh sample of 18 rows per family (216; no screened row or group), R1 and R2 as four
  fresh subagents (two packets each, R2 in another order), a fresh R3 on the 3 splits.
  - **13 errors: 6.02% [3.24, 10.07]; weighted 8.33% [3.86, 13.54]. P1 FAIL, P2 FAIL, P3 PASS → FAIL.**
  - By family: `sentfin` 3, `sumedit` 3, `wands` 3, `args` 1, `csqa` 1, `isarc` 1, `w2c` 1; `copa`, `poem`, `sms`,
    `snips_rel`, `snips_sel` 0.
  - Agreement with gold: R1 94.0%, R2 95.4%, majority 94.0%; R1–R2 raw 98.6%, κ .985 (one model family; the
    reviewers agree with each other far more than with gold). No defect flags.
- **Error pattern (counts only; ids stay on node A):** `sentfin` gold neutral, reviewers positive (3 of 3, plus the
  screen's one); `sumedit` gold "consistent", reviewers found an unsupported detail (3 of 3, all in the Shakespeare
  domain); `wands` at the Partial boundary (2 gold Irrelevant, 1 gold Exact, reviewers Partial); the other four are
  single items. 9 of the 13 errors are label-boundary cases in three families.
- Limitation: AI adjudication, not human annotation.

## Deviations and incidents

- The four stage-R reviewers ran as background subagents (in parallel) instead of foreground; each was fresh,
  answered one packet blind and wrote only its answer file. The screen reviewer and R3 ran in the foreground.
- Run `b1` stopped at the build: one SNIPS file encodes emoji as UTF-8 surrogate pairs (fixed in `17359493`).
- The upload copy of the G0 receipt omits the suite statistics (kept on the node), so no Index-derived count leaves
  the private directories.

## How tracks could use IB1

Because IB1 is not release-safe, a successor trained on it cannot be released. For experiments: add
`m6/ib1/ib1.train.jsonl` as one block against a matched-token control, screened with HT-DEV v2; leave out `w2c` and
`isarc` to measure transfer from the other families alone; measure Index effects privately on frozen packages; the
C1 recheck comes first for any C1-scored model.

**Candidates for a separately preregistered IB1-r2 (coordinator):** drop or re-filter `sentfin` neutral rows,
SummEdits' Shakespeare domain and WANDS Partial rows (9 of the 13 errors), then a fresh decisive review with the same
thresholds. Three rounds now point the same way (HR2 6.0%, HR2-r2 4.5% pooled but 8.0% weighted, IB1 6.0%):
crowd-labelled boundary classes give AI reviewers about 5–6% disagreement, at the edge of the 5% bar. A next round
should prefer objectively checkable constructions (IB1's `poem`, `snips_*`, `sms`, `copa` had 0 errors in 72 rows) or
filter boundary classes before sampling.
