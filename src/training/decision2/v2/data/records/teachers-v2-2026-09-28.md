**Selection (preregistered rule: lowest scaled Brier, < 0.005 prefers Lux):** AutoJev for Choice,
Noul and Score. Per the teacher policy AutoJev targets ship as a **separate file**; release
candidates adopt them only with a recorded coordinator decision, because AutoJev's public pipeline
includes SFT rows generated with a closed OpenAI model (the reason Jev itself is excluded is baking a
competitor's outputs into our weights). Own-Lux targets are produced for the whole pool as the clean
default. Caveat: public teachers may have seen some public v2 sources in their own training (Decider
lists WinoGrande and CommonsenseQA), which inflates their screen on those parts; the screen is still
the right basis for choosing targets on exactly these prompts.

## Canonical own-Lux targets

- **A0 TRAIN:** `m2/teachers/lux1/A0-train.canonical.jsonl` `dd160420…` (7,455 rows; see the arm
  registry for cross-checks against the decoder and 9B files).
- **RP-v2** = every non-A0s row of mx-v2-full-L ∪ mx-v2-short-M (121,461 rows; rows `fb0326de…`,
  prompts `f81b537c…`), so targets cover every recipe (S ⊂ M ⊂ L). Produced in nested waves:
  wave 1 = S recipes (28,501), wave 2 = M recipes (55,976), wave 3 = L remainder (36,984), then a
  256-prompt repeat run. Each wave is converted by `v2.data.m2.targets` (model identity, attested
  revision, validated runtime, prompt digest per row) and uploaded to
  `m2/teachers/lux1/rp-v2/wave<k>.{targets.jsonl,report.json}`; hashes are appended below as waves land.
- **AutoJev RP-v2** was queued after Lux on node B; the queue was cut before it started (M3a moved
  AutoJev to node A). The node-A qualification failed its preregistered frozen-autotune gate, so no
  AutoJev target exists ([record](m3a-autojev-qualification-2026-09-28.md)).
