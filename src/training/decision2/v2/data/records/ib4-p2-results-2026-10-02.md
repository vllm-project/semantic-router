## What was tried

Phase 2 admitted one source after the source audit of amendment 3: **ATOMIC (2019)** train (CC BY 4.0,
`atomic_data.tgz` `f2277994…`), built as `atom`. It is a Noul twin family targeting the HellaSwag-like gap: is the
inference a likely intent / precondition / effect of the event? Yes is the event's own inference; no is another event's
inference with no shared content word.

The failure is the construction, not contamination. Many crowd-written intents are abstract ("to be hurtful", "to be
reliable", "to help himself"), so another event's intent is often a plausible intent of this one. Neither run is
published; both stay on node A (`p2-a3`, `p2`) as private evidence.
