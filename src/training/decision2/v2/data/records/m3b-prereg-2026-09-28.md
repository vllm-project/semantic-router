  itself without teacher targets.
- **Reported:** tokens by type, language, Score level count, pool and source; English share; own-Lux and
  AutoJev coverage per recipe with the rows lacking targets listed.
- **C1:** every recipe source is checked by name and upstream against the eval track's C1 source registry
  (`v2/eval/records/sealed-c1-source-registry-2026-09-28.json`, registry and rejected entries); any match is
  removed before publication.
