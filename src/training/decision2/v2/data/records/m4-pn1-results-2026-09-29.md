  | N4XF | .708 | | |

  The direction holds in every language. This mirrors mlx-diag PAWS-X, where N4XF says yes .762 and Nox 1.0 .663.

  - es and fr TRAIN hold natural pairs only: their swap group failed the gate and was dropped.

## For decoder Milestone 6 (an N5BN replicate + PN1)

- **Arm:** `m4/pn1/arms/pn1.train.jsonl` (4,888 rows, about 0.55M tokens), added to the N5BN recipe as a whole-group
  block.
  - At about 2% of a 29M-token mixture, a 2–3× repeat is reasonable. ja, zh, de and ru carry most of the swaps.
  - Matched control: the N5BN replicate at equal tokens without PN1.
- **Readout:** `m4/pn1/dev/pn1.dev.prompts.jsonl` through the same runners, or `v2.dec.eval_rows` on
  `pn1.dev.jsonl`.
  - The baseline is the table above: N4XF .708 / .791 and Nox 1.0 .653 / .841 (yes-rate / accuracy).
  - A successful arm should cut the near / twin yes-rates without lowering hop.
- **Out-of-family check:** mlx-diag PAWS-X stays the out-of-family check. PN1 shares no source or sentence with it.
- **Honest limits:**
  - es / fr / ko / ar TRAIN volume is small.
  - The dev es / fr swap rows are out-of-construction for the arm.
  - The judge-filtered labels have no blind QA yet.
