## Blind review (prereg §4)

- **Stage S (screen):** 10 TRAIN rows per family (50) from pass 2, one fresh `claude-opus-5-5-max` reviewer with
  `dq/reviewer-ib3.md`: **5 disagreements** (`fdial2` 3 → dropped; `mqa` 1, `phiu` 1; `esci`, `wpd` 0).
- **Stage R (decisive):** a fresh sample of 54 rows per family (216; no screened row or group); R1 and R2 fresh
  subagents (two packets each, R2 in another order), in parallel in the foreground; R3 (fresh) on the 2 splits.
  - **29 errors: 13.43% [9.18, 18.71]; weighted 10.65% [6.39, 15.42]. P1 FAIL, P2 FAIL, P3 FAIL → FAIL.**
  - By family: `phiu` 12, `wpd` 8, `esci` 8, `mqa` 1.
  - Agreement with gold: R1 85.6%, R2 86.6%; R1–R2 raw 99.1%, κ 0.98 (one model family); 23 low-confidence answers
    per reviewer; 2 items flagged as defects by both (garbled problem text).
- **Error pattern (counts only; ids stay on node A):** `phiu` 12 and `wpd` 8 are all gold phishing that both reviewers
  read as legitimate — blocklist labels on URLs (and homepage titles) that carry no visible phishing signal; `esci` 6
  gold Exact read as not exact and 2 gold Irrelevant read as exact (annotation boundary); `mqa` 1 key both reviewers
  rejected.
- Limitation: AI adjudication, not human annotation.

## For the coordinator (an IB3-r2 would need its own preregistered round and a fresh review)

- `mqa` is the clean family (1 / 54; formula-verified keys, per-option twins, G4 pass). A round built from `mqa` alone
  (or with a second formula-verifiable maths source) would likely pass.
- Phishing URL labels from blocklists are not checkable by a reader: an r2 needs page evidence (title, form target,
  brand-domain mismatch) with a preregistered "visible signal" filter applied to both classes, or a licensed
  phishing-email pair. Phishing email still needs a licensed legitimate-email corpus.
- `esci` needs a stricter Exact definition (or an Exact vs Irrelevant pair where the irrelevant product shares no
  query attribute), verified on a screen before the decisive review.
- Grounding needs a source whose supported and unsupported responses share one style and whose labels are claim-level
  (FaithDial's chit-chat labels and HaluEval's generated answers both failed); candidates still to check: WiCE
  (CC BY-SA, claim-level), FactCheck-GPT, ExpertQA claim-level labels.
