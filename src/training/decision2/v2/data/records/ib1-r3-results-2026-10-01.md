## Fresh blind review: PASS

**Sample.** 216 items from the pass-4 TRAIN file, 24 per family across 9 families. The amendment-2 top-up rule raised
the quota from 22 to 24. Every row sampled in rounds 1 and 2 was excluded together with its group: 775 key rows.

**Reviewers.** Four fresh subagents (`claude-opus-5-5-max`), all run in the foreground (`run_in_background: false`)
with the round-1 instructions:

- R1 and R2 each answered both packets, in different item orders;
- R1 and R2 agreed on all 216 items, so no R3 was needed.

| Measure | Result | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| Errors | 3 / 216 = 1.39% (exact 95% CI 0.29–4.01%; Wilson 0.47–4.00%) | ≤ 5% and upper ≤ 8%: **P1 pass** |
| Population-weighted | 2.14% (bootstrap 95% CI 0.00–5.36%) | ≤ 5%: **P2 pass** |
| Per family | at most 1 / 24 | no family's exact lower bound > 5%: **P3 pass** |

**Errors:**

- `args`: the argument text is only "Summary". Both reviewers flagged it as a defect.
- `sentfin`: gold negative, reviewers positive.
- `w2c`: the reviewers preferred the direct answer.

All 3 rows are dropped. Agreement with gold was 98.6% per reviewer, and κ between reviewers was 1.0. Unanimous
agreement on binary items is plausible, but it means the review did not measure item difficulty.

**Disclosed limitation.** 71 of 5,182 `args` TRAIN rows have an argument of three words or fewer, mostly portal
headings, and two of them are just "Summary". They were left in place, because changing data after the review would be
tuning.
