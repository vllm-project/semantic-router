Rules: prereg §8, with **amendment 3** (below). Builder `v2.data.m2.mixtures` at `ae34de446`, pools
`pools-mx-v2.json` (final v2 TRAIN files + A0s + v1 arms, per-row native tokens from
`v2.data.m2.row_tokens`). Manifests (`<recipe>.ids.jsonl`: id, pool, source, native tokens) and

files by id (`m2/arms/<ARM>/train.jsonl`, A0s from `v2/arms/A0s/`, v1 arms from `v2/arms/`).

## Amendment 3 (before any recipe was built)

The prereg shares (Score 30%, H3 15%, H5 15%, H1 15%, E11 10%, G 15%) cannot meet the English cap
(≤ 60% of recipe tokens) because A0s is 84% English and H1/H3/E11 are English-only. Shares of the
v2 portion are therefore: **full** H5 0.28, H6 0.14, G6 0.10, v1 A6g+A6h 0.06, H3 0.12, H1 0.08,
E11 0.07, G2 0.12, G4h 0.03; **short** (rows ≤ 1,024 native tokens, no H3) H5 0.33, H6 0.15,
G6 0.11, v1 A6g+A6h 0.06, H1 0.10, E11 0.08, G2 0.13, G4h 0.04. Per-source cap 8% of recipe tokens.
Whole groups only (twins, coverage groups and G4 pairs stay intact). Order
`sha256("mx-v2:<variant>:" + group_id)` is budget-independent, so **S ⊂ M ⊂ L**.

13.5M tokens per GPU-hour at 0.6B; scale by each tier's own measured throughput. Replay: for own-1.0
continuation add 10% R2 (`v2/replay/R2/<tier>`) as D7 guides; the recipes do not include it.
