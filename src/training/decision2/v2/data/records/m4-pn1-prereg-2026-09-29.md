## 2. Arm specification

- **Task:** native Noul through the house renderer (`training/model/decision_model.segments`).
  - Instructions: English, one of 8 fixed phrasings.
  - Options: English No/Yes (`noul_options("en")`, keys `false` / `true`).
  - State: the two native-language sentences in one of 5 fixed English layouts. The mlx-diag `Sentence 1:` /
    `Sentence 2:` layout is not used.
  - Phrasing, layout and sentence order are chosen from the row id hash, independently of the label.
- **Constructions** (the row `family`):
  - **`pn-hop` (yes):** two distinct same-language Tatoeba sentences that share a direct translation, one pair per
    pivot. Dropped if they are equal after normalization (NFKC, casefold, punctuation and space removed) or their length
    ratio is > 1.6.
  - **`pn-near` (no):** two same-language Tatoeba sentences with character-bigram Jaccard ≥ 0.30 and length ratio
    ≤ 1.6. They must not be equal after normalization, must have no shared translation within two link hops, and
    their English translations (where both have one) must differ after normalization. Each sentence is used at most
    once.
  - **`pn-name` (rule):** sentences naming two persons from a fixed per-language name list.
    - Coordination swap (A CONJ B → B CONJ A, with Korean particle allomorphy fixed): yes.
    - Role swap (the names exchanged in place; neither coordinated nor comitative): no.
  - **`pn-twin` (generated):** for one seed Tatoeba sentence, the generator writes a pair of minimal edits.
    - The no edit swaps two words or phrases so the meaning changes while (nearly) every word is kept.
    - The yes edit keeps the meaning by reordering or by at most one synonym.
    - An edit is kept only if its character-unigram multiset Jaccard with the seed is ≥ 0.80, it differs from the
      seed after normalization, and its script matches the language.
    - Seeds favour longer sentences: ≥ 8 words for es/de/fr/ru/ar, ≥ 5 for ko, ≥ 16 characters for ja/zh.
- **Label check:** the judge sees a fixed English prompt with the pair in random order (not the house renderer) and
  gives P(same meaning).
  - A yes row is kept if P ≥ 0.5, a no row if P < 0.5.
  - The edited sentence of every `pn-name` / `pn-twin` row must also pass a judge fluency check (P(natural and
    grammatical) ≥ 0.5).
  - Disagreement rates per family × language before filtering are reported as a label-noise estimate.
- **Balance:** labels are exactly 50/50 per language, in both TRAIN and the dev slice.
  - Strata within a language: construction group (natural = hop + near; swap = name + twin) × same-multiset flag
    (character-unigram multisets equal) × overlap bin. The bins are on character-bigram Jaccard: [0, .3), [.3, .5),
    [.5, .7), [.7, .85), [.85, 1].
  - Each stratum takes equal yes and no counts: the smaller class count, in seed-hash order.
  - There are no random-pair negatives and no negation-insertion edits. Every no row is a near-miss or a swap.

## 3. Size and language mix

- **TRAIN targets** (20,000 rows in total):

  | ja | es | ko | zh | de | fr | ru | ar |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 4,400 | 3,200 | 2,800 | 2,800 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 1,400 | 1,400 |

  - The swap group should reach ≥ 50% of rows in ja / ko / zh / es and ≥ 40% elsewhere.
  - A language short after filters takes all of its balanced rows. There is no redistribution, and the shortfall is
    reported.
- **Dev slice:** 300 rows each for de, es, fr, ja, ko, zh and 100 each for ru, ar (2,000 in total). Its construction
  mix is within 10 points of that language's TRAIN mix.

## 4. Isolation

- **Source item group:** a pair's Tatoeba sentence ids plus every sentence within two translation-link hops, in any
  language. For twins and name swaps the key is the seed sentence.
- **Drawing the slice:** dev keys are drawn first, per language in `sha256("pn1-dev:" + key)` order.
- **Filtering TRAIN:** a TRAIN candidate is dropped if its ids intersect a dev neighbourhood, or if any of its
  sentences is equal after normalization to, or a near-duplicate of (character 4-gram containment ≥ 0.8), a dev
  sentence.
- **Row ids and checks:**
  - Group ids are `m4pn1:<namespace>:<sha24>`; row ids are `m4pn1-<family>-<sha24>`.
  - Dev rows have split and evaluation role `select`.
  - `v2.data.freeze isolation` runs PN1 TRAIN against PN1 dev, and PN1 against every existing partition in
    `existing.json`, plus the H7/H8 slices.

## 5. Audits

Each audit passes, or is resolved by dropping whole groups; everything is recorded.

- **A1 — overlap with evaluation panels.**
  - House lexical scan (`v2.data.overlap`) against PI-v4 (full and quarantining subset, union), extended with HT-DEV
    and Score5-DEV. Score5-typed-DEV is not built yet and is screened when it exists.
  - House embedding scan on node B.
  - A new short-text sentence scan, because the house scans skip text leaves under 20–40 characters. Each PN1 sentence
    is checked against sentence segments of every gold-free panel prompt file (mlx-diag, typed-final, CSS15, public
    231, typed DEV, SELECT, CAL / CAL698, HT-DEV, Score5-DEV) and against all of PAWS-X (7 languages, all splits) and
    English PAWS.
    - Hit: exact after normalization (≥ 6 characters), or character 4-gram containment ≥ 0.8 in either direction for
      sentences of ≥ 12 characters.
  - A hit against an evaluation or benchmark role drops the whole group. Hits against held-out training slices only
    are disclosed.
- **A2 — C1:** the registry check in §1 plus the custodian recheck request.
- **A3 — dev exposure:** the short-text scan of the dev sentences against the rows N4XF trained on
  (`m4-xl-full-29m`) and against the own-1.0 corpora (A7) on the nodes. Hits are dropped from dev.
- **A4 — shortcuts.**
  - The house `v2.data.shortcut` gated views (state-removed, option-only).
  - A pair overlap learner: character-bigram and character-unigram-multiset Jaccard, same-multiset flag, word
    Jaccard, length ratio, normalized edit distance and containment in both directions, fed to logistic regression
    and a depth-4 tree over 5 group-disjoint folds.
  - Single-sentence views: first sentence only and second sentence only, hashed character 1–4-gram logistic
    regression.
  - Pass: accuracy ≤ majority + 0.05 per language (n ≥ 200) and pooled, on TRAIN; dev is reported.
  - On a failure, the failing feature's bins are rebalanced once. If the view still fails, that family is dropped for
    that language.
- **A5 — mix:** counts per language × family × label, token totals from `freeze`, and a per-sentence script check.
- **A6 — label QA (diagnostic, not gating):** one label-blind reviewer labels 20 dev rows per PAWS-X language (120).
  Agreement with gold is reported.

## 7. Dev-slice validity check

- **Models:**
  - N4XF, `model_sha256 11b5ca1c…fad0ad` (the node-B soup; see the M5 records).
  - Nox 1.0, `llm-semantic-router/Decision-1.0-Nox-4B@0bb833504965c0eabdb9630b7bbd385cb2fe5cd4`.
- **Formal runtime settings:**
  - Node B GPU3–4, image `dbe5f32b`, `--max-length 16384`, no truncation.
  - Each run gets a `cp -a` copy of the frozen `cache-frozen-mlx` (`65d7d38f…`).
  - The container is offline and sees only the gold-free prompt file.
  - Runners: `v2.dec.infer_dec` for N4XF, as in the M5 node-B reference run; `v2.dec.infer_1p0` for Nox 1.0 (the
    decoder's shared renderer).
- **Scoring:** predicted yes means P(true) > 0.5. Reported: yes-rate, accuracy and gold rate per language and per family,
  pooled over the six PAWS-X languages (primary) and over all eight.
- **VALID iff,** on the pooled six languages:
  - (i) yes(N4XF) − yes(Nox) ≥ 0.03, with the paired group-bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0 (10,000 resamples); and
  - (ii) the lower bound of the 95% CI of yes(N4XF) − gold is > 0.
  - Per-language results and the ja / ko direction are reported but do not gate.
- **No tuning to the models:** the slice is never selected or edited after model outputs are seen. If it is INVALID,
  that is recorded and the slice is not tuned. The arm is still published, flagged.

## 8. GPU budget and stop rules

- **Budget:** ≤ 1.5 GPU-h on node B GPU3–4, counted as wall-clock × GPUs, including loading, preflights and
  validation.

  | Item | GPU-h |
  | --- | --- |
  | Generation | ≤ 0.6 |
  | Judge | ≤ 0.45 |
  | Validity | ≤ 0.25 |
  | Embedding scan | ≤ 0.05 |

  - A timed preflight on 256 items per model sets the volumes.
  - Generation stops if the cumulative total passes 1.15 GPU-h. Validity always keeps its share.
  - Lease owner entries: `owner.data` in `gpu3.lock` / `gpu4.lock`.
- **Stop and record:**
  - a source download that can't be pinned;
  - a model that fails its licence check at the pinned revision;
  - a failed preflight (load, parity with the M5 N4XF mlx-diag answers on a 20-prompt smoke, or throughput);
  - an A1 hit that points at panel leakage in the source itself;
  - any budget overrun.
