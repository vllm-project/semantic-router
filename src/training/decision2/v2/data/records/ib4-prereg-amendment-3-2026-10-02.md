## 2. Family `atom` (Noul, twins; cell = ATOMIC dimension)

- **Input.** ATOMIC `v4_atomic_trn.csv`, rows with split `trn`; events with a `___` blank are skipped. Dimensions:
  xEffect, xWant, xNeed, xIntent, oEffect, oWant (the event-structured "what follows / what preceded" ones; the
  one-word xAttr / xReact / oReact are not used). Annotations are pooled per (event, dimension); "none" is dropped.
- **Specific inferences only.** An inference is eligible if it has at least 2 words and occurs for at most 2 events in
  its dimension. One eligible (dimension, inference) per event, picked in hash order.
- **Rows.** State {event, aspect (a fixed phrase per dimension), inference}; the question is fixed.
  - Yes: the event's own inference.
  - No: the inference of the next event in a per-dimension hash cycle (up to 49 steps ahead) whose inference shares no
    content word with this event or any of its inferences, and whose event shares no content word with this event.
  - So every inference is yes for its own event and no for at most one other event.
- **Balance and size.** Yes = no inside every dimension; at most 6,000 events (12,000 rows) before audits. Group = the
  normalized event.
- **G4** hypothesis field: `inference`.
- **Phase 2 dedupes and isolates against phase 1.** It also dedupes against IB1-r3 / IB2 / IB3-r2 (§2.0) and the
  published phase-1 TRAIN / DEV. PI-ib4 adds the phase-1 DEV file; G7 adds it as a partition.

Every audit of prereg §3 runs from scratch in run `p2`. That includes IX1 against the full private panel with the planted
control 200 / 200, and a C1 recheck spec `r4-ib4p2.json`. Publication goes to `m6/ib4/p2/`. If `atom` fails G4, phase 2
publishes nothing.
