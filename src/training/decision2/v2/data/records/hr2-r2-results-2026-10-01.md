## 2. Licence decision

VitaminC and Allegro Reviews are **dropped** (TRAIN and DEV) under the rule "no NC, and no ShareAlike that is
incompatible with Apache-2.0 model releases and data-card attributions; drop if uncertain":

- CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0 require Adapted Material to carry the same (or a BY-SA-compatible) licence, and Apache-2.0 is not
  one; whether trained weights are Adapted Material is unsettled, so compatibility is uncertain.
- VitaminC's pinned `LICENSE` grants "the license terms described on the applicable Wikipedia article pages", with
  CC BY-SA 3.0 only as a fallback (the card says `cc-by-sa-3.0`): no single licence to credit.
- Allegro's pinned HF snapshot has no licence file or tag (CC BY-SA 4.0 is stated only in the GitHub README); the
  texts are marketplace users' reviews with no documented rights chain; CC BY-SA 4.0's database-right clause could
  extend ShareAlike to the whole HR2 dataset (HR2 carried 35% of the train split).

HR2-r2 keeps HelpSteer3 (CC BY 4.0), ETHICS (MIT) and PRM800K (MIT); none is ShareAlike. `license-registry-hr2.json`
lists only these three (the dropped sources moved under `_not_in_hr2_r2`), so `v2.data.freeze` would refuse a row of
a dropped source. This is stricter than the 2026-09-28 22:20 card policy (Apache-2.0 label, CC BY-SA training data
credited on the card); the same question applies to CC BY-SA sources already in released mixtures (coordinator).

## 5. Fresh blind review (amendment 4 §F)

288 TRAIN rows (48 per family, gold-stratified 24 / 24, one per group), none sharing a row or group with round 1 (135
round-1 rows and 146 groups excluded); two packets of 144 per reviewer order. R1 and R2: four fresh

- The weighted error is driven by `hs3_pref` (43% of TRAIN rows). All 7 of its errors had medium or low reviewer
  confidence (no error at high / high); 6 have overall margin 2 (6 / 37 margin-2 rows vs 1 / 11 margin-3 rows).
- `prm_step`: two first-error (−1) steps the reviewers judged correct, and one rated-correct opening step they
  rejected.
- Over both rounds: HelpSteer3 preferences 8 / 72 (11.1%), PRM800K 4 / 72 (5.6%), ETHICS 6 / 288 (2.1%). Round 1
  had shown `hs3_pref` at 1 / 24, which is why the analysis estimate (2.88%) was optimistic.
- Limitation, as before: the reviewers are one model family (AI adjudication, not human annotation); several
  HelpSteer3 disagreements may be reviewer preference rather than label error, which this design cannot separate.

## 6. Incidents

- **Recheck iterations (G2):** above. Amendment 4 §E's end condition ("must find no group") was met by repeating the
  drop and re-scan; nothing else changed and no row had been sampled. Runner `fd3cd9e40`; recorded in the state file
  before sampling.
- **A duplicated SSH invocation** of the iteration loop: one copy kept running on the node, a second copy found the
  recheck directory already moved and stopped at its first check. The surviving loop's shell was stopped after it had
  started `audits`; that stage finished normally and nothing ran twice. Later long stages ran detached (`nohup`).
- The first tooling commit was rejected by the `black` hook (formatting only) and re-committed.

## 7. How tracks should use HR2-r2, and options for the coordinator

- HR2-r2 is **not release-safe**: as with round 1, a successor trained on it cannot be released. For experiments it
  supersedes round 1 (no ShareAlike source, the constructions behind 9 of round 1's 13 errors removed, pooled review
  error 4.51% vs 6.02%): add `m5/hr2/hr2.train.jsonl@16ea6cf7` as one block against a matched-token control
  (~10.6M native tokens), screened with HT-DEV v2; `hr2.dev.jsonl` per family is a diagnostic only. Item 8 needs the
  custodian C1 recheck first.
- Candidates for a separately preregistered round (not done here, as amendment 4 requires): the ETHICS families
  (6 / 288 over both rounds) and PRM800K (4 / 72) are the cleanest; HelpSteer3 preferences are the open problem
  (8 / 72), which a human adjudication of the disagreements, or a stricter margin rule tested on fresh rows, could
  resolve.
- Licence: the stricter ShareAlike rule used here is a coordinator question for the CC BY-SA sources in released
  mixtures.
